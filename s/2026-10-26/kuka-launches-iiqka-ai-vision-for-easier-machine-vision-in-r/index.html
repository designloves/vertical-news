KUKA launches iiQKA.AI Vision for easier machine vision in robot cells

Lower integration effort for AI vision is relevant to anyone automating assembly and inspection lines in automotive and bearing plants.

Robot maker KUKA introduced iiQKA.AI Vision on 26 October, an AI-based industrial image-processing solution. KUKA says it is designed to simplify the integration of machine vision into robotic automation.

No performance figures were available in the coverage we found. As with other vendor launches, claims about setup time or accuracy should be tested on your own parts and lighting conditions.

For manufacturing engineers, the direction matters more than the product: vendors are packaging vision models so that cells can be configured with less specialist programming, which could shorten commissioning of inspection steps.
