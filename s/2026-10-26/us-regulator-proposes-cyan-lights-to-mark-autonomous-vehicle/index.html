US regulator proposes cyan lights to mark autonomous vehicles

Lighting and marking rules for driverless cars feed directly into ADAS and autonomy requirements and test specifications.

Electrek reported on 24 October that the US vehicle safety regulator NHTSA has proposed using cyan-coloured lights to identify vehicles operating autonomously. The idea is to let other road users and first responders tell when a car is driving itself.

The item is a proposal, so details such as placement, brightness and timing could change after public comment. We only saw the headline summary, not the rule text.

Engineers working on autonomy and homologation should watch the final text, since a mandatory external signal would become a new function to specify, validate and test across lighting, software and vehicle-state logic, and could influence similar discussions in UNECE.
