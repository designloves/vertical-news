Marvell to buy Lumentum in an all-stock deal valued at $115 billion

A bet that AI data centres will be limited by connectivity, which affects who supplies the hardware behind AI services.

Marvell Technology agreed on 2 November to acquire optical-components maker Lumentum. The deal is all stock at a fixed ratio of 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share, giving Lumentum an implied enterprise value of $115 billion. The final value will move with Marvell's share price. Closing is expected in the first half of 2028.

Investors reacted coldly: Marvell shares fell 13% and Lumentum fell nearly 19% on the day. Both companies had risen sharply during the AI boom. Marvell's CEO said combining electrical and optical technologies would create 'a new category of company', aimed at the growing connectivity needs inside AI clusters. CNBC did not report on regulatory review.

For European buyers of AI infrastructure, the deal signals further consolidation among a small number of component suppliers, at a time when data-centre build-outs are already straining capital.
