Palantir's US commercial revenue passes its US government revenue for the first time

One of the clearest public data points on enterprise AI adoption turning into revenue.

Palantir reported third-quarter revenue of $2.31 billion, up from $2.18 billion expected by analysts and nearly double last year's figure. US commercial revenue rose 148% to $984 million and overtook US government revenue of $953 million for the first time. Net income was $1.13 billion.

The company raised its outlook: it now expects about $2.65 billion in Q4 revenue, around $8.53 billion for 2026, and US commercial revenue above $3.56 billion, up from $3.42 billion. Shares dipped about 3% in extended trading.

For product leaders, the shift suggests that companies are paying for AI that is tied into operations and data, not only for chat assistants. European firms weighing similar platforms should note that this growth is reported for the US market only.
