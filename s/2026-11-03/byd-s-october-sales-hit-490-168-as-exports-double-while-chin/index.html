BYD's October sales hit 490,168 as exports double while China demand stays weak

Shows where BYD's growth is coming from, which is increasingly the export markets where European makers compete.

BYD sold 490,168 vehicles in October, up 11% from a year earlier, its sixth straight month of growth, according to Reuters. Growth slowed from 17% in September.

Overseas sales were 170,194 units, up 102.8%, which implies roughly 320,000 sold at home, where demand is sluggish and price competition is intense. Reuters adds that BYD's third-quarter profit growth of 28% missed forecasts, and that it could face new EU curbs on hybrid imports under a China-EU arrangement.

For European OEMs and suppliers, the numbers point to continued pressure from Chinese exports in the EU and other markets, just as trade rules on hybrids may change.
