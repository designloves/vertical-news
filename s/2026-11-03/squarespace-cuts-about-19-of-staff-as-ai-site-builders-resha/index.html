Squarespace cuts about 19% of staff as AI site builders reshape web design

Shows how prompt-based builders are squeezing template-driven website platforms and the designers who work on them.

Squarespace is laying off about 19% of its global workforce, roughly 300 people, according to TechCrunch, which says The Verge reported it first and a spokesperson confirmed. The company calls it a restructuring to simplify operations and sharpen priorities.

TechCrunch ties the move to the rise of 'vibe-coding' tools that produce working front-end designs from short prompts. It points to Lovable's $400 million Series C in August at a $13.3 billion valuation and Google's Stitch UI-design tool. Squarespace has its own AI tools and has announced Forma, an AI-native platform with a waitlist. The company went private in 2024 in a $7 billion deal.

For designers and front-end developers, the signal is that value is moving away from layout templates toward brand, content strategy, accessibility and the quality of what AI generates.
