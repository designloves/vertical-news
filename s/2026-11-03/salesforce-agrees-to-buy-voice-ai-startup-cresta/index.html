Salesforce agrees to buy voice-AI startup Cresta

Another sign that big software vendors are buying AI agents for customer service instead of building them.

Salesforce announced a definitive agreement on 2 November to acquire Cresta, a voice-first customer-experience platform. Cresta's voice agents can handle complex calls from start to finish, and its tools coach human agents in real time and analyse conversations. Named customers include Marriott, Comcast and Alaska Airlines. Salesforce did not disclose a price in its announcement, and the deal is expected to close in the first quarter of its fiscal 2028, subject to approvals.

Salesforce says the aim is to bring 'the customer, the conversation and the outcome' into one place, building Cresta's capabilities into its service platform. Cresta will keep supporting the contact-centre and telephony systems its customers use today.

For product strategists, voice is moving from a separate add-on to a built-in layer of service software. Companies selling into support teams should expect bundled AI voice features to reset buyer expectations on price.
