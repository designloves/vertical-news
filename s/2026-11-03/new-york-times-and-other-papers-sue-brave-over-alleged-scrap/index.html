New York Times and other papers sue Brave over alleged scraping for AI customers

Adds legal risk to the data supply chain behind AI search and model training, including for European users of these tools.

The New York Times sued Brave in federal court in Manhattan on 2 November. The Daily News, the Chicago Tribune and the Orlando Sentinel filed a similar suit the same day. The complaints say Brave scraped copyrighted articles and supplied them to AI companies, naming Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI and Cohere as customers.

According to TechCrunch, the Times says Brave built an index of more than 30 billion pages with a crawler that publishers could not identify or block. The suits also challenge a browser feature that bypasses paywalls. The Times seeks an injunction and damages. Brave had not commented when the article was published.

These are allegations, not findings. For product teams that license search or retrieval data from third parties, the case is a reminder to check where that data comes from.
