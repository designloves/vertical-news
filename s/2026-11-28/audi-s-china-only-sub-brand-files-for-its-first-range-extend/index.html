Audi's China-only sub-brand files for its first range-extender model, an E7X with a 1.5-litre generator

Even premium German brands are adding range extenders in China, a technology that EU hybrid rules treat differently.

China's industry ministry MIIT has published approval filings for a range-extender version of the Audi E7X, the first such model from the China-only 'AUDI' sub-brand that SAIC Volkswagen builds. The fully electric E7X went on sale in late April on a 900-volt platform, with 100 or 109 kWh CATL batteries and up to about 750 km CLTC range.

In the new version, a 1.5-litre petrol engine rated at 105 kW works only as a generator. The rear motor drops from 300 kW to 244 kW peak (165 kW continuous), and top speed stays at 205 km/h. Kerb weight is listed at 2,560 to 2,590 kg. The battery uses ternary cells from SAIC-CATL, but capacity and total range have not been published. Large Chinese range-extender SUVs typically carry 40 to 65 kWh.

For powertrain teams, the filing shows how quickly an EV platform can be turned into a range extender for markets where charging worries persist.
