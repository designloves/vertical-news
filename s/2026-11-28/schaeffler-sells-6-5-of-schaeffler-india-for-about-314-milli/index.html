Schaeffler sells 6.5% of Schaeffler India for about 314 million euros to cut debt

The bearing and drivetrain group is turning a strong Indian unit into cash for its balance sheet.

Schaeffler placed a 6.5% stake in Schaeffler India with institutional investors on 26 November. It sold about 10.16 million shares at 3,493 rupees each, raising net proceeds of about 314 million euros after taxes and fees. The parent group's holding falls from 74.13% to about 67.63%, and Schaeffler says it will keep at least two-thirds of the Indian company.

The money will go towards reducing net debt and strengthening the equity ratio. Schaeffler India runs five plants and sells FAG and INA bearings and LuK clutch products, competing with SKF, NSK and Timken in a growing market. The sale follows an October notice that Schaeffler was reviewing its holding, and comes weeks after the group announced 3,460 job cuts and five site closures in Europe.

For buyers and engineers, the move underlines India's growing role as a bearing production and engineering base for European groups, while the European footprint shrinks.
