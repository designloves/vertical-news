SKF Vertevo passes its record date as an SKF executive warns about Chinese bearing makers

SKF's automotive bearing business becomes a separate listed company on Monday.

Friday 27 November was the record date for SKF's spin-off of its automotive business, SKF Vertevo. Shareholders receive one Vertevo share for every five SKF shares of the same series, and trading on Nasdaq Stockholm starts on 1 December under the tickers SKFVER A and SKFVER B. The Swedish financial supervisor approved the listing prospectus.

The new company starts from a difficult position. In the first nine months of 2026, Vertevo's sales were about 18.0 billion kronor, down from 19.1 billion, with an operating loss of 707 million kronor. It targets an adjusted operating margin above 6% in the medium term and 8% or more in the long term, and does not expect to pay a dividend for 2026. On the same day, Bearing News reported an SKF executive in Sweden warning about Chinese competition, uncertain energy prices and Europe's technology dependence.

For OEM purchasers, Vertevo becomes a separate counterpart for wheel-end, driveline and e-axle bearings, with its own cost pressure and pricing logic.
