Stellantis will stop its Sochaux, Rennes and Mulhouse plants for weeks over December and January

Weak European demand and Chinese competition are turning into lost production days for French plants and their suppliers.

Stellantis is again halting production for several weeks at three French plants, citing a difficult European market and aggressive competition. Mulhouse stops on 4, 7 and 8 December and from 23 December to 11 January. Rennes stops from 18 December to 5 January, possibly longer. Sochaux stops on 3, 4, 7 and 8 December and from 19 December to 11 January 2027.

The same sites already paused for five to 15 days each this autumn, two of them because of EV battery shortages. Sochaux plans to end its night shift in January, affecting about 500 temporary workers, and unions in Rennes fear half a shift could go in 2027. Stellantis registrations in wider Europe fell 8% to 144,786 cars in October, according to ACEA data.

For suppliers in the Peugeot, Citroën and Opel chain, including drivetrain and bearing suppliers, the stoppages mean lower call-offs through the turn of the year and more uncertainty in 2027 planning.
