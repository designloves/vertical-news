Stellantis will stop its Sochaux, Rennes and Mulhouse plants for weeks over Christmas

Long winter stoppages at three core French plants show how hard Chinese competition and high costs are hitting European volume makers and their suppliers.

Stellantis is pausing production again at three French plants, blaming a difficult European market with new, aggressive competitors and a complicated geopolitical situation. Mulhouse, which builds the Peugeot 308 and 408, stops on 4, 7 and 8 December and then runs no shifts from 23 December to 11 January. Rennes, which builds only the Citroën C5 Aircross, stops from 18 December until 5 January, possibly longer. Sochaux, home of the Peugeot 3008 and 5008, stops on four days in early December and from 19 December to 11 January. About 500 temporary jobs there will go as the night shift is dropped in January.

Each plant already stopped for 5 to 15 days in the autumn, partly because of EV battery shortages. ACEA figures show Stellantis registrations in wider Europe fell 8% in October, and its market share dropped to 12.8%.

Suppliers to these lines should plan for lower December call-offs. The group is also leaning on partners Leapmotor and Dongfeng to fill spare European capacity, and Rennes is due to build Voyah cars from 2028.
