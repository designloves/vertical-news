BMW launches the Neue Klasse i3 in China from 249,900 yuan, with fixed prices and up to 1,028 km CLTC range

It is BMW's main answer to falling China sales, and fixed nationwide pricing breaks with the usual dealer discounting there.

BMW launched the long-wheelbase Neue Klasse i3 sedan at Auto Guangzhou on 27 November. There are three versions. The 30L, at 249,900 yuan (about 37,500 dollars), uses a 77 kWh CATL LFP pack rated at 708 km on the Chinese CLTC cycle. The 40L, at 279,900 yuan, has an 86.9 kWh pack of large cylindrical NMC cells rated at 802 km. The 50L, at 319,900 yuan, has 113.4 kWh and up to 1,028 km. All use a single sixth-generation eDrive motor with 235 kW, and deliveries start in January 2027.

The car is 4,864 mm long on a 3,005 mm wheelbase and has a standard 43.3-inch Panoramic Vision display. BMW sells it at one fixed price nationwide and skipped the usual refundable deposit stage. It follows the long-wheelbase iX3, launched on 25 November. BMW's China deliveries fell 21.8% in the first nine months.

The i3 brings the platform European engineers know from the iX3 to China, with China-specific packs and range figures to match local rivals.
