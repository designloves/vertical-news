Guangzhou motor show opens with 1,106 vehicles, 64% of them new-energy models

China's product cadence keeps setting the pace that European carmakers and suppliers must answer.

The 24th Guangzhou International Motor Show opened on 27 November at the Canton Fair Complex and runs until 6 December. More than 1,000 brands fill over 240,000 square metres. Of the 1,106 vehicles on show, 707 are new-energy vehicles, or 63.9%, up from 58% last year, and 115 models are making their first appearance.

Hyundai used the show to unveil a compact electric SUV show car for China, alongside a concept and a facelift. BYD with its Denza, Yangwang and Fang Cheng Bao brands, NIO, XPeng, Li Auto, Leapmotor, Zeekr and Xiaomi all brought new cars. Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, Toyota, Honda and Nissan are also exhibiting.

For European engineers, the share of new-energy launches and the number of premieres are a reminder of how short Chinese development cycles have become. Many of these models, or their platforms, are expected to reach Europe within one or two years.
