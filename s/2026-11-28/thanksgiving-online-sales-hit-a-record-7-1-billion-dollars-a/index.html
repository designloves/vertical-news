Thanksgiving online sales hit a record 7.1 billion dollars, and shoppers arriving from AI tools convert 74% better

AI assistants are becoming a real sales channel, and product pages need to work for them.

Adobe Analytics says US shoppers spent a record 7.1 billion dollars online on Thanksgiving Day, up 10.4% on last year and above Adobe's own 6.9 billion forecast. Mobile devices accounted for 63.2% of spending. Adobe expects 12.9 billion dollars on Black Friday and a record 15.1 billion on Cyber Monday.

The striking figure is AI traffic. Visits to US retail sites from AI-powered sources such as chat assistants rose 162% year on year. Those visitors were 74% more likely to buy and 72% more likely to add items to their cart than other visitors. Adobe's data covers more than one trillion visits to retail sites.

For product and UX teams, this supports investing in structured product data, clear pricing and fast checkout that work well when an assistant has already done the research. It also means analytics should separate AI-referred visitors, who arrive further along in their decision than typical search traffic.
