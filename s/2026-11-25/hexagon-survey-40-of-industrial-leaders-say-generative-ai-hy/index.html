Hexagon survey: 40% of industrial leaders say generative AI hype is pulling money from factory autonomy

Precise measurement and inspection data are named as a bottleneck for autonomous production, which is where NDT and metrology fit.

Hexagon's Autonomy Unlocked study, carried out by Opinion Matters, surveyed 3,600 operations technology leaders in six countries and six industries, including automotive. 24% said their operations run at high or full autonomy today, and 61% expect to get there within ten years.

40% said hype around generative AI is diverting investment from operational autonomy, and 22% named a lack of trusted measurement or precise data as a barrier. Self-reported maturity ranged from 16% in the UK to 29% in China.

The survey is commissioned by a vendor and based on self-assessment. It is still relevant because Hexagon now includes Waygate Technologies, the NDT business it consolidated from 1 November, and is pitching inspection and measurement data as the base for autonomous factories. For test and quality teams, that argument supports funding inline inspection and data quality before AI layers.
