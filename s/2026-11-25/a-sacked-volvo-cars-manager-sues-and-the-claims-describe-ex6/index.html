A sacked Volvo Cars manager sues, and the claims describe EX60 production problems

The case points to possible ramp-up trouble on Volvo's EX60 and its Torslanda battery line.

A former senior manager at Volvo Cars has taken the company to Gothenburg District Court, Carup.se reports, citing Göteborgs-Posten. The claims include more than 2.5 million kronor in compensation, 300,000 kronor for discrimination and 150,000 kronor in damages, plus a monthly salary of over 200,000 kronor while the case runs.

The filing describes problems around the EX60. A supervisor allegedly called production results catastrophic, and the claims mention trouble at the South Carolina plant and a reported six-month delay in battery assembly at Torslanda. Volvo says the manager downloaded confidential files and does not comment on the case itself.

These are allegations in a lawsuit, not court findings. For suppliers and engineers working on the EX60 and Volvo's SPA3 platform, they are a sign to watch call-off schedules and ramp-up plans closely.
