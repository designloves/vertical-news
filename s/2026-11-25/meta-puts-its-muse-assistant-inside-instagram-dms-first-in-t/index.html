Meta puts its Muse assistant inside Instagram DMs, first in the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore

Assistants are moving into the messaging threads where people already share links and plans, which is a pattern for any app with a chat surface.

Meta is rolling out its Muse AI assistant inside Instagram direct messages on iOS and Android. Users can chat with Muse in a thread to get context on a post, place or product, catch up on missed conversations, and act on shared posts, for example adding a café to a travel itinerary or finding a deal on shoes. These chats also appear in the main Muse app.

Muse can also sort saved posts and reels into folders and turn a recipe video into a shopping list or travel clips into an itinerary. Meta says nothing is shared or sent without the user's approval, and users can ask Muse to forget information or undo actions. The launch covers the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore. The EU is not included.

For product teams, it is a clear example of an assistant placed inside an existing social flow rather than in a separate app, with approval steps for every action.
