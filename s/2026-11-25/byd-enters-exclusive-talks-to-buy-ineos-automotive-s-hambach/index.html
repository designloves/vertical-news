BYD enters exclusive talks to buy Ineos Automotive's Hambach plant in France

A second European car plant for BYD would bring Chinese EV production and its supplier needs closer to European engineering hubs.

BYD says it is in exclusive talks to buy the Hambach plant in north-eastern France from Ineos Automotive. The site was built by Mercedes-Benz for Smart and later sold to Ineos, which has used it for its Grenadier off-roader. The deal is subject to works council consultation, and no price, timetable or model plans have been given.

BYD says it wants to protect local jobs and create work for regional suppliers. The company already has a plant in Hungary, and a French site would add capacity inside the EU at a time when tariffs and the new hybrid quotas make imports from China more expensive or limited.

For European suppliers, a BYD-run Hambach would be a new local customer, but also a sign that Chinese brands plan to compete with EU-built cars on home ground.
