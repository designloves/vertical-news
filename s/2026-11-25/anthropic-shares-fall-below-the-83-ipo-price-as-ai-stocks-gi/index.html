Anthropic shares fall below the $83 IPO price as AI stocks give back Monday's gains

Anthropic's share price has become the market's reading on what public investors will pay for frontier AI, and it affects funding for the AI tools product teams rely on.

Anthropic's shares dropped as low as $76 by midday on Tuesday, below the $83 IPO price and nearly 12% down from Monday's close. The stock opened at $111.50 on its Nasdaq debut on 19 November. Trading below the offer price is usually read as a bad sign, because underwriters tend to price an IPO below where they expect it to trade.

The fall came on a weak day for AI stocks in general. Nvidia lost more than 7% by midday, giving back part of the 30%-plus jump it made on Monday after Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO.

Observers see Anthropic as a test case for the sector. A sustained slide could make private investors more cautious on AI start-up valuations and push back other planned AI listings. For teams that build on Claude, nothing changes in the product for now, but the pressure on the company to show revenue growth rises.
