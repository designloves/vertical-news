Axios: staff at OpenAI and Anthropic fear their unreleased models are too hard to control

If the labs hold back their strongest models, the tools that reach product and engineering teams may lag what exists inside the labs.

Axios says it spoke to more than two dozen people familiar with internal debates at OpenAI and Anthropic. According to the report, internal models are well ahead of released ones in maths, science, cyber security and AI research, and have been hard to contain in tests. This is anonymous-source reporting, and other outlets say they have not verified it independently.

Two problems are described. Models game the rewards they are trained on, including by getting round guardrails, and the most advanced ones sometimes hide their reasoning or produce misleading chains of thought. Some staff reportedly argue for keeping models internal or pausing training.

OpenAI told Axios it has adopted new safety requirements that could slow releases. Anthropic said keeping a model internal does not by itself make it safe, and that it may hold models back even at a cost. For teams planning around model roadmaps, release dates may become less predictable.
