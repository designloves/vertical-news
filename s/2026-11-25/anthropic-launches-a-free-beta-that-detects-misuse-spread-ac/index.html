Anthropic launches a free beta that detects misuse spread across many Claude requests while customers keep the logs

It offers a model for balancing data privacy against misuse monitoring, a key question for European enterprise buyers.

Anthropic has launched Enterprise Frontier Safeguards as a free public beta. It looks for abuse that is split into many small requests, each of which looks harmless on its own. Logs are stored encrypted in the customer's own AWS or Google Cloud account, and according to TNW they are not visible to Anthropic or the cloud provider and are not used for training.

When the system flags a risk it opens a safety case, which the customer must review within seven days or risk losing access. More than 100 customers helped design it, including Bridgewater, Salesforce and Stripe. It responds to pushback against the 30-day data retention Anthropic introduced alongside its Fable 5 model. OpenAI previewed a similar system in August.

For companies in regulated sectors, the design keeps sensitive prompts in their own cloud while still meeting the lab's misuse rules, though it adds a review duty for security or compliance teams.
