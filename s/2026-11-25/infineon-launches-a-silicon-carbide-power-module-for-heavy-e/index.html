Infineon launches a silicon-carbide power module for heavy electric trucks, designed for 1.2 million km

The module targets megawatt charging and long truck lifetimes, which shape e-axle and inverter validation for truck makers.

Infineon has introduced the HybridPACK HD, a silicon-carbide module for traction inverters in heavy electric trucks and buses. It handles 500 to 1,200 amps using 1,400-volt CoolSiC MOSFETs, and the housing is designed for battery voltages up to 1,500 volts DC, aimed at vehicles using megawatt charging.

Infineon says the module is designed to last more than 1.2 million kilometres and over 80,000 operating hours. It uses the company's .XT interconnect technology, direct water cooling and laser-welded connections. The company has also made its first SiC devices on 300 mm wafers in Villach, with about 2.3 times as many chips per wafer, and plans about 1.1 billion euros of investment there by 2034.

For truck makers such as Volvo Group and Scania, higher voltages and long-life targets affect inverter design, thermal modelling and the durability tests needed to sign off electric drivelines.
