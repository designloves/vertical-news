Daimler Truck takes a minority stake in Swedish foundry-tech firm SinterCast and plans AI process control

It ties a Swedish materials specialist to a major truck maker and brings machine learning into how heavy-duty engine blocks are cast.

Daimler Truck will take a minority stake in Stockholm-listed SinterCast through new shares and warrants, combined with a licence for its South African foundry Atlantis Foundries. The size and price of the stake were not disclosed, and SinterCast shareholders vote on the deal on 18 December. Atlantis plans to install its first SinterCast System 4000 Plus at the start of 2027.

SinterCast's process control is used for about two-thirds of the world's compacted graphite iron, a strong cast iron used for heavy-duty engine blocks and heads. The partners plan AI-based predictive process control that combines SinterCast's metallurgy models and software with production data from Atlantis.

For engineering teams, this is a practical case of industrial AI where the value comes from closing the loop between material models and real foundry data. SinterCast says the proceeds will fund growth and acquisitions and add recurring licence revenue.
