Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and a restricted Mythos 5.2 at lower prices

A cheaper, faster top-tier model changes the cost calculus for AI-assisted coding, design prototyping and engineering workflows.

Anthropic launched two new models on Monday. Claude Fable 5.2 is generally available and is aimed at demanding work in engineering, mathematics, robotics, coding and agents. Mythos 5.2 stays limited to vetted organisations in the company's life-sciences and cyber verification programmes, as earlier Mythos versions were because of their security capabilities.

Anthropic says the models produce output about 30% faster than the previous generation and cost about 20% less. Fable 5.2 is priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50. CNBC reported no benchmark results, so the capability claims are not yet independently checked. Anthropic positions its Opus 5.5 model, released in September, as the better fit for everyday tasks.

The release is the first frontier launch since the company's chief executive called in September for slowing AI development; a product executive described that as pacing rather than pausing. Anthropic's amended IPO filing reportedly shows $33.2 billion of revenue for the first nine months of the year, and the listing could happen as soon as next week. For product teams, the practical points are the price cut and the speed gain for agent-style workloads, which are where long, token-heavy tasks cost the most.
