Google makes Pathways, the system behind Gemini training, generally available on Google Cloud

Gives outside teams access to the fault-tolerant training approach Google uses itself, though only on TPUs with JAX.

Google announced general availability of Pathways on Google Cloud, in a developer blog post that starts a series. Pathways is the distributed runtime Google uses to train its own large models. A developer writes one Python program for the whole job, and the runtime decides where each part runs on TPUs, moves data and recovers from failed machines.

Google had said in April 2025 that customers would get access. The post gives no pricing, region list or named customers, and no new performance figures. It cites Google's earlier Gemini 2.5 report, which credited the design with recovery at about 97% throughput after hardware failures, and a July test that recovered 48 TPU chips in under two minutes.

Pathways runs JAX on TPUs only, and the post does not say whether PyTorch or GPUs are supported. Teams training their own models should treat it as an option mainly if they are already on TPUs.
