Nvidia's Halos SAFE links simulation, XiL and fleet data for AV validation, and Porsche targets 90% fewer test drives

A concrete vendor framework for simulation-led sign-off, with familiar validation names: dSPACE, AVL, TÜV Rheinland.

Nvidia launched Halos SAFE, a safety assurance framework that connects simulation, hardware- and software-in-the-loop, on-road testing and fleet monitoring into one evidence chain. dSPACE, IVEX and TÜV Rheinland are collaborating on it, and JLR, Mercedes-Benz, Volvo Cars, AVL, Foretellix and Synopsys are part of the ecosystem. New Cosmos 3 Nano variants generate synchronised multi-camera and lidar data for closed-loop simulation.

On the OEM side, Mercedes-Benz extends its partnership for L3 and L4 work. Volvo Cars adopts Hyperion and the Alpamayo stack. VW Commercial Vehicles shows its ID. Buzz AD. Bolt plans a European robotaxi programme on at least 25,000 future Lucid vehicles, starting data collection in Madrid in 2027. Porsche says its data flywheel for VW Group could cut repeated physical test drives by up to 90% and development cycles by up to 85%. Both figures are expectations, not results.

For virtual validation teams, the signal is that toolchain vendors and certifiers are lining up around simulation evidence that counts toward homologation. The open question is how regulators will weigh synthetic data against physical miles.
