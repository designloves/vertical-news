Anthropic previews Claude Duet, a proactive agent that keeps working when you leave

A clear reference design for proactive, background agents: an inbox of updates, a task list and approval gates instead of a chat box.

Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet, a standalone app (iOS, Android and web) where Claude comes to you with what needs attention, completes tasks you assign, and keeps going on its own cloud computer after you close the app. It runs on Opus 5.5 and uses the same memory, skills and connectors as regular Claude. It is rolling out in waves to US Pro and Max subscribers from a waitlist, with Team and Enterprise to follow. There is no extra charge, but background work counts against plan limits.

The interaction model is worth studying. There is one main thread plus topic channels with shared memory, a daily brief built from mail and calendar, an Updates feed that flags decisions, a Tasks tab, and a Make tab holding finished deliverables as editable files. Duet asks before sending messages, paying, signing or deleting, and only the user can mark a task done.

This puts Anthropic up against OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse in the always-on assistant space. For product teams, it shows the patterns converging: proactive surfacing, explicit permission checkpoints, and outputs treated as artifacts rather than chat replies.
