Factory agents go to early access, as Mercedes, Audi and Schaeffler show simulation-first workflows

Shows where European OEMs and suppliers are using AI in engineering right now: aero CFD, production twins and actuator twins.

Nvidia released its Factory Operations Blueprint (FOX) in early access on GitHub. It is a template for an always-on factory manager agent plus specialist agents for inspection, root-cause analysis and reporting, connecting to CAD, PLM, simulation and IT/OT systems. Siemens estimates about 20% cost savings from FOX-based root-cause agents and a 40% cut in inspection calibration time at Erlangen.

Several automotive examples were shown. Mercedes-Benz runs STAR-CCM+ on GPUs for faster aerodynamic simulation and uses vision agents to verify windshield assembly. Audi tests battery-production concepts in an Omniverse twin before the equipment exists. Schaeffler is integrating digital twins of humanoid-robot actuators with Isaac simulation to reduce physical testing. Dassault's Nemotron-based virtual companions work with CATIA and SIMULIA agents to design and virtually test vehicle doors, and Autodesk brings simulation-trained surrogate models into Alias.

Schaeffler's actuator-twin work is closest to bearing and drivetrain component validation. It's worth watching whether it extends to bearing-level models.
