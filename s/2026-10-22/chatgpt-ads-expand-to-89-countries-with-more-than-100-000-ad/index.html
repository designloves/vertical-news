ChatGPT ads expand to 89 countries, with more than 100,000 advertisers in eight months

Conversational ads are becoming a real channel, and a new surface where your product could be recommended, or advertised against.

OpenAI added 26 markets to ChatGPT Ads, including 14 more in Latin America plus Nigeria, South Africa, Serbia, Ukraine, Pakistan and Bangladesh, for 89 countries in total. Ads appear only on the Free and Go tiers. They are labelled and kept separate from answers, and withheld from under-18s and from sensitive topics such as health and politics. OpenAI says more than 100,000 advertisers have signed up in about eight months, with buying through partners or a self-serve Ads Manager.

OpenAI says the next phase brings new formats, objectives and measurement. European markets were added earlier in the pilot.

For PMs and growth teams, this makes 'answer-engine' visibility a paid and organic channel in its own right. It also sets a precedent for freemium AI products: ads on the cheap tier, a clean experience on paid tiers.
