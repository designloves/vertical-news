Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro lands near the top of independent rankings, with open weights promised by 4 November

A cheap, open-weight model close to proprietary frontier systems gives European teams a self-hostable option for sensitive engineering data.

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro on Friday. It is a mixture-of-experts model with about 2.8 trillion total parameters, 104 billion active per token, and a one-million-token context window. The company says it will publish the full weights by 4 November.

Reports say API pricing is about $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Moonshot's headline result is 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, but that comes from the company's own testing and several comparison tests were built in-house. Artificial Analysis reportedly ranks the model fourth overall and first among open-weight models, still behind Anthropic's Opus 5.5. Moonshot itself says its offensive-cyber ability is below top proprietary models.

If the weights ship as promised, companies that cannot send design or test data to a US cloud could run a near-frontier model on their own hardware. Check the licence terms and run your own evaluations before relying on the vendor's benchmark figures.
