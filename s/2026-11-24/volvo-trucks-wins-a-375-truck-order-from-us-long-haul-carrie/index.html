Volvo Trucks wins a 375-truck order from US long-haul carrier E&O ONE

A large diesel long-haul order shows where Volvo Group's North American volume still comes from while European fleets move to electric.

Volvo Trucks North America said on Monday that E&O ONE, a long-haul carrier based in Elgin, Illinois, has ordered 375 new Volvo VNL trucks. About 125 are being delivered in 2026 and the other 250 are scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. The carrier already runs an all-Volvo fleet across the 48 contiguous US states.

The trucks are the VNL 860, Volvo's largest sleeper cab, with the Volvo D13 engine rated at 455 horsepower. A six-battery setup powers a parking cooler and heater, so drivers can rest without idling the engine. E&O ONE is building a new headquarters in Elgin and plans to expand its Huntley service centre in 2027 with a 40,000 square foot building and 150 trailer spaces.

For engineers at Volvo Group and its suppliers, the order is a reminder that diesel long-haul trucks remain the core of North American demand. Idle-reduction features such as battery-powered cab climate systems are where electrification is entering these fleets first.
