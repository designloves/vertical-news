Google's AI Mode can now draw on Drive, Wallet and the Gemini app for personal answers

Search is turning into a personal assistant built on users' own files and finances, which changes how people find and judge information.

Google said on Monday that Personal Intelligence in Search's AI Mode now connects to Google Drive, Google Wallet and the Gemini app. It already worked with Gmail, Calendar and Photos. Each connection is optional, and the feature can be turned off at any time.

Google's examples include using Drive documents and spreadsheets to plan a project or a tax season, and using Wallet data to track savings goals or get more from credit card rewards. Google stresses that AI Mode can only read account information and cannot move money. Ideas started in the Gemini app now carry over to AI Mode and back. Google also plans a small personalisation icon under answers that shows which context was used, and says AI Overviews will lean more on recent searches.

For UX teams, the transparency icon is the pattern to watch: personal answers need a visible trace of their sources to earn trust. For product teams, it is another sign that users will expect assistants to work across their own data without switching apps.
