SKF India launches the 'Vertevo' brand and adds bearing capacity at Haridwar and Pune

New lines for deep-groove ball and tapered roller bearings point to continued automotive and industrial demand in India.

SKF India's board met on 3 November, took the September-quarter results on record and approved two capacity projects. It also launched a new brand identity, SKF Vertevo, which the company says draws on racing heritage and links to the future of mobility. The report does not give financial figures for the quarter.

At Haridwar, two lines will be added to the four existing deep-groove ball bearing lines, in two phases: up to INR 247 million from December 2026, and up to INR 379 million from March 2027. At Pune, which has six tapered roller bearing channels, a channel upgrade with extra machines (up to INR 347 million) starts in May 2027, followed by one more production line (up to INR 431 million) from July 2027. All of it is funded from internal accruals.

This concerns the Indian subsidiary, not the SKF group. It still shows where bearing makers are placing new volume capacity, in regional production close to growing vehicle and industrial markets.
