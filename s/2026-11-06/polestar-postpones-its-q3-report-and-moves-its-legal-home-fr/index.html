Polestar postpones its Q3 report and moves its legal home from Sweden to the Netherlands

A delayed quarterly report and a legal restructuring at a Volvo Cars-linked brand will draw attention from investors and suppliers in Gothenburg.

Polestar said it is postponing its third-quarter and nine-month 2026 report, which was due on 5 November, to allow more time for the quarter-end close. A new date will be given "in due course". No reason beyond the close process was reported.

The same day, Privata Affärer, citing Dagens industri, reported that Polestar will convert from a Swedish to a Dutch company. This concerns legal structure only. Headquarters in Torslanda outside Gothenburg and the group's functions are reported to stay where they are, and the article gives no reason for the change.

Polestar earlier this year ended production of the original Polestar 2 in China and announced that Polestar 3 production would move to Volvo Cars' US plant. For Swedish suppliers and engineering partners, the main questions now are how the delay and restructuring affect volume plans and development spending.
