Schaeffler's full nine-month report: bearings division margin rises, E-Mobility still deep in the red

The split by division shows industrial bearings improving while the EV business absorbs about €240 million of charges.

Schaeffler published full nine-month results on 3 November, following preliminary figures in October. Revenue was €17.48 billion, down 1.1% (0.4% at constant currency). EBIT before special items rose 6.5% to €794 million, a 4.5% margin. Free cash flow before M&A was negative €40 million, though the third quarter alone produced €260 million. Full-year guidance was confirmed: revenue €22.5–24.5 billion and an EBIT margin of 3.5–5.5%.

The Bearings & Industrial Solutions division grew 1.1% at constant currency to €4.8 billion, with a margin of 8.9% against 7.8% a year earlier. E-Mobility revenue rose 4.6% to €3.87 billion, but the division posted an EBIT of minus €571 million (minus 14.8%). About €240 million of impairments and provisions, mainly on surplus production equipment and portfolio streamlining, were booked in the third quarter. Powertrain & Chassis revenue fell 4.6% at constant currency. Greater China fell 7.9%, while Asia/Pacific overall grew 7.3%.

The numbers suggest the industrial side is steady while EV component capacity is being trimmed to fit demand. Employee count is down 2.5% since the end of 2025, to 108,004.
