OpenAI hires Anthropic's Claude Code product chief Cat Wu to run its Core Products

The person who shaped the leading AI coding agent now moves to its main rival, a signal of where agent products are heading.

Cat Wu, who led product for Claude Code and Cowork at Anthropic, announced on 5 November that she is joining OpenAI to lead product management for its Core Products group. She reports to Tibo Sottiaux, and will work with Nick Turley on enterprise products and Ashley Alexander on consumer ones. Her last day at Anthropic was 30 October. OpenAI has not said what her exact portfolio or start date will be.

At Anthropic she oversaw Claude Code's expansion into the browser and, by March 2026, a multi-agent code-review feature. Cowork handled file and tool tasks for non-developers. Claude Code is the closest counterpart to OpenAI's Codex. In her own announcement she said she wants to build agents that help people and teams while keeping users in control.

For product and design teams, the move shows how much competition now centres on agent products rather than models alone. Product leadership, with questions about oversight, review and trust in autonomous tools, is becoming a key hire. Expect more of the interface patterns pioneered in coding agents to reach general office work.
