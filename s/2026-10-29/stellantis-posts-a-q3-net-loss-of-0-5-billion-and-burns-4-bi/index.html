Stellantis posts a Q3 net loss of €0.5 billion and burns €4 billion of industrial cash

One of Europe's largest vehicle makers is barely profitable at the operating level, which keeps pressure on suppliers and on spending for new vehicle programmes.

Stellantis published its third-quarter 2026 results in a filing with the US regulator. Net revenues were €37.6 billion, up 1.0% on a year earlier. The group reported a net loss of €0.5 billion and adjusted operating income of €0.3 billion, a margin of 0.7%. Industrial free cash flow was negative €4.0 billion, while industrial available liquidity stood at €42.7 billion. The company reaffirmed its guidance.

By region, revenue in the enlarged Europe region rose 3% and South America 3%. North America fell 3%, Asia Pacific fell 7% and Middle East & Africa dropped 19%. Earlier in October the group had already indicated that consolidated shipments for the quarter were about 1.3 million vehicles, down 1%.

For suppliers and engineering teams, a thin margin and heavy cash burn at a major customer usually mean tougher price negotiations and closer scrutiny of development budgets. The figures are company-reported and unaudited.
