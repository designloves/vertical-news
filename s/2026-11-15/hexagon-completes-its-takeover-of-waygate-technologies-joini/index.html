Hexagon completes its takeover of Waygate Technologies, joining metrology and NDT

One supplier can now cover CT, ultrasonic testing and coordinate measurement, which may simplify inspection set-ups in bearing and drivetrain plants.

Hexagon has closed its acquisition of Waygate Technologies from Baker Hughes, about seven months after the deal was announced in April. Closing was reported on 3 November and wider trade coverage followed this week. Waygate becomes part of Hexagon's Manufacturing Intelligence division.

Waygate brings industrial computed tomography, field radiography, ultrasonic testing and remote visual inspection. Hexagon adds coordinate measuring machines, portable measuring arms, scanners and metrology software. Michael Bellamy stays on as Waygate's chief executive, and Hexagon says existing projects, service agreements and orders continue without interruption. Neither company has given a deal value or headcount in the coverage.

For manufacturers of bearing rings, gears and shafts, the combination points towards inspection lines where CT and ultrasonic defect data sit in the same software as dimensional measurement. That could make it easier to link internal defects to geometry and to feed both into quality analytics and AI-based defect detection.
