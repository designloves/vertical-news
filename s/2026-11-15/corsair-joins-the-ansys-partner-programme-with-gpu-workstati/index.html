Corsair joins the Ansys partner programme with GPU workstations and servers sized for simulation

More vendors are tuning hardware for GPU-based CFD and FEA, which changes how test and simulation departments budget compute.

Corsair has joined the Ansys Technology Partner Program, now run by Synopsys, with its CORSAIR PRO line of workstations, servers and clusters. The company says it will build configurations matched to the Ansys application, model size, licensing and budget of each customer.

The FlexPrime workstations are aimed at pre- and post-processing and single-node studies, with the top model claimed at over 400 teraflops in single precision. FlexGrid GPU servers target multi-GPU Fluent runs and on-premises use of SimAI, Ansys's AI surrogate tool, with nearly 500 teraflops in double precision claimed. Clusters cover distributed CPU solves. These are vendor figures.

At the SC26 supercomputing conference in Chicago from 15 to 20 November, Corsair will show Fluent results streamed into Nvidia Omniverse, and Synopsys will present GPU-accelerated Fluent benchmarks on Nvidia Blackwell. For automotive CAE teams, the practical question is whether on-premises GPU machines now beat cloud or CPU clusters on cost per simulation for CFD and AI surrogates.
