Replit's agent can now buy things and run Google Ads campaigns, with a human approving spend

App-building tools are moving from making software to operating the business around it, and the approval step is the design pattern to copy.

Replit has given its coding agent two new powers. With agentic commerce, built on Stripe's Link and Visa, the agent can make one-time purchases for US users on Core and Pro plans. It prepares checkout in an isolated browser and stops for the owner to approve the final total, and shell access and general browsing are switched off during payment.

With Agentic Ads, available to Pro users worldwide, the agent drafts ad copy and images, suggests daily budgets and sets up conversion tracking across Google Search, YouTube, Display and Discover. Nothing is spent until a person approves. Users can connect an existing Google Ads account or fund a Replit-managed one from their Replit credits. Replit says the number of builders connecting Stripe has grown ninefold since May. It has not published pricing or spending caps.

For product managers, the release shows how agent features are being packaged: narrow, high-value actions, a sandboxed execution environment and an explicit confirmation step at the moment money moves.
