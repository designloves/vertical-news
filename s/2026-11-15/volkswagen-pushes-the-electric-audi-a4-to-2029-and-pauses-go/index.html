Volkswagen pushes the electric Audi A4 to 2029 and pauses Golf EV work as its SSP platform slips

Delays to VW's next software-defined platform affect supplier schedules, validation programmes and electrical-architecture choices across the group.

Volkswagen is delaying another model on its 800-volt Scalable Systems Platform (SSP). According to Autocar, production of the electric Audi A4 e-tron has moved to 2029 at the earliest, a year later than planned. Audi asked for changes to make the car closer to the petrol A4, including separate footwells for front and rear passengers, which means moving the battery, and a 48-volt electrical system in place of the planned 12-volt one.

Reuters separately reported an internal memo pausing Golf EV development work in Wolfsburg. In the memo, VW brand chief Thomas Schäfer says the company cannot afford the work right now. VW Group posted a €5.9 billion operating loss in the third quarter. The works council says the Golf EV is still expected in 2030. Software for SSP is being developed with Rivian through the RV Tech joint venture.

For suppliers and test engineers, a 48-volt change this late reworks component specifications and validation plans, and it shows how much platform timing now depends on software readiness.
