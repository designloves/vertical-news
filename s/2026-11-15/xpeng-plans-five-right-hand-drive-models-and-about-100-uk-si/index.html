Xpeng plans five right-hand-drive models and about 100 UK sites to lead the premium EV market by 2028

Chinese brands are now targeting the premium segment in Europe with driver assistance as a selling point, not just low prices.

Chinese carmaker Xpeng says it wants to lead the UK premium electric car market by 2028. It plans five right-hand-drive models within 12 months, starting with the L03 and G9L, each offered as a battery-electric car and as a Kunpeng extended-range version with a small combustion engine acting as a generator.

The company plans about 100 retail and service sites during 2027, three flagship stores in London and Manchester within six months and around 20 dealer and leasing partners. A national parts hub is meant to supply stores within 24 hours. Its NGP driver assistance system, built on its VLA 2.0 model, is planned for the UK in 2027, and Google Play integration is due early that year.

Reaching the goal would mean overtaking BMW and Tesla in UK premium within two years. For European OEMs and suppliers, the plan shows extended-range drivetrains and AI-based driver assistance becoming key competitive features in a market with no EU tariffs on Chinese cars.
