Investors and a 115,000-signature petition push Nvidia's board to consider Jensen Huang's return

Pressure on the board makes Nvidia's leadership, and its spending on open models and AI start-ups, even less certain for the coming weeks.

Altimeter Capital and Atreides Management, which together disclosed about $2.13 billion in Nvidia shares at the end of June, published a letter on Friday asking Nvidia's board to weigh every leadership option, including Huang's return. They did not demand that he be reinstated straight away. Instead they asked for a careful CEO search that waits for the facts, and said interim CEO Colette Kress can run the company in the meantime.

A separate 'Stand with Jensen' petition had passed 115,000 signatures by Saturday afternoon. TechCrunch reports that signers include nearly 6,000 people who say they are current Nvidia employees, along with Elon Musk, Tim Sweeney and Garry Tan. These affiliations are self-reported. The petition asks for an independent investigation and more public information. Huang resigned on 12 November during a board inquiry into his personal relationships, and he has agreed to testify before Congress. The board has not responded publicly.

AI researcher Nathan Lambert argues that Huang's exit is a setback for US open-weight models, because Nvidia's Nemotron work depended on his personal backing. Teams that build on Nvidia's open models and tools should watch whether that funding holds.
