OpenAI and Databricks connect ChatGPT and Codex to companies' governed data

Enterprise AI is being sold through the data platform a company already uses, with its permissions and budgets, not as a separate tool.

Databricks has widened its partnership with OpenAI. Business users can now query governed Databricks data from ChatGPT through a Genie plugin. The plugin applies each user's existing permissions and shared metric definitions. Engineers can run OpenAI's Codex through Databricks' Unity Gateway, which manages credentials, budgets and audit logs. Teams can also build custom agents on GPT models, including the GPT-6 family and the open-weight gpt-oss models, inside Databricks.

Databricks did not publish pricing. It says OpenAI is one of its largest customers and that every Databricks engineer is getting Codex. It also claims OpenAI models lead its OfficeQA benchmark for document work, but it gave no scores.

For product strategy, this follows a familiar pattern. Model vendors are going after enterprise customers through the platforms that already hold their data and access controls. Getting a first AI agent approved can then be a configuration change rather than a new procurement.
