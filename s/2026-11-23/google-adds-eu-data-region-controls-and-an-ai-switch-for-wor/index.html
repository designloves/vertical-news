Google adds EU data-region controls and an AI switch for Workspace Studio

Data residency plus an admin on/off switch for generative AI is what many EU companies need before enabling agent workflows.

Google says the core features of Workspace Studio, its tool for building automated flows and agents, now follow US or EU data-region policies, and that support is generally available. A new admin control decides whether users in a data region may use generative AI. When it is off, Studio's AI steps, flow generation from plain language and Skills are disabled; both this control and the Gemini in Workspace setting must be on for AI to work.

In the same update, client-side encrypted Google Docs gain real-time co-editing in beta, with encryption keys staying under the customer's control. Gemini Enterprise also made third-party data stores generally available, including Asana, Airtable, GitLab, Stripe, Zapier and Linear.

For product leaders, this is a pattern worth copying: AI features in business software increasingly ship with region-level data residency and a separate, explicit AI toggle for administrators, so compliance teams can approve the product before approving the AI.
