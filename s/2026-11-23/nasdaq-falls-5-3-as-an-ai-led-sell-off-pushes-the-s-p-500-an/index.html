Nasdaq falls 5.3% as an AI-led sell-off pushes the S&P 500 and Dow into a correction

A sharp turn in AI sentiment can tighten budgets for AI projects and data-centre build-outs in 2027.

US stocks had one of their worst days in years on Friday. The Nasdaq Composite fell about 5.3% to 22,617.73, wiping out its gains for 2026. The S&P 500 dropped 3.7%, its seventh losing session in a row, and both it and the Dow ended at least 10% below their recent highs, the usual definition of a correction. The VIX volatility index closed at its highest level since April 2025.

Nvidia fell about 14% after a Reuters report that regulators are examining possible accounting problems, on top of the scandal around former chief executive Jensen Huang. Anthropic, a day after its debut, lost about 9% and ended just above its IPO price. Analysts also pointed to expected Federal Reserve rate rises, the Democrats' midterm gains and growing local opposition to new data centres.

For product leaders, this is a signal to watch AI vendor pricing and funding, not a reason to change plans overnight. Startups that rely on cheap capital and suppliers building capacity may get more cautious.
