Musk says Tesla cars will stay on AI4 while the new AI5 chip goes to Optimus first

It shows how Tesla is splitting compute between vehicles and robots, with no hardware upgrade date for older cars.

Replying to a Tesla owner on X on Sunday, Elon Musk said cars will stay on the AI4 computer, which he says is powerful enough to drive better than a human, while the AI5 chip goes to the Optimus humanoid robot first. He put AI5 at about 12 times AI4's compute, and in another reply estimated about a tenfold gain in overall hardware performance, adding that memory bandwidth is still a limit.

He gave no date for when cars might move to AI5 or when cars with the older HW3 computer might get an upgrade. AI4 cars now get FSD (Supervised) v15, while HW3 cars stay on a lighter v14 branch. Tesla signalled this direction in April, when it said AI5 would go to Optimus and data centres.

For automotive engineers, the decision shows a carmaker choosing to keep a fixed in-car compute platform and improve driving through software, while new silicon goes elsewhere. It also leaves the HW3 retrofit promise to early FSD buyers unresolved.
