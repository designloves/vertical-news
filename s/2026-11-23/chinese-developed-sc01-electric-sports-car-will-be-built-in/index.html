Chinese-developed SC01 electric sports car will be built in Italy from May 2027

Chinese EV makers keep moving assembly into the EU, which changes the homologation and supplier picture.

FIPA (Fabbrica Italiana Produzione Autoveicoli) will assemble the SC01 electric sports car for Europe at an existing industrial site in Italy's Marche region. The car was developed by Chinese start-up Tianjin Gongjiangpai Auto Technology. FIPA, led by entrepreneur Wenyu Sun, says it is the only authorised importer and maker of the SC01 in the EU and will handle European homologation.

Buildings will be renovated from January to March 2027, lines installed and homologation completed by May, and production will start at the end of May 2027. The first phase has 2,000 square metres of production space and about 50 staff, rising to 285 jobs by the end of 2030 and 24,000 square metres within two years. The Chinese version has 320 kW all-wheel drive, 560 Nm and weighs just under 1.4 tonnes. No volume target or European price was given.

It is a small project, but it fits a wider pattern: Chinese-developed cars assembled in Europe to get around tariffs and rules of origin, often with local firms responsible for type approval.
