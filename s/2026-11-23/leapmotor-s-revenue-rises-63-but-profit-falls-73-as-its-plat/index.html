Leapmotor's revenue rises 63% but profit falls 73%, as its platform launches in Europe under Hongqi

The fastest-growing Chinese brand in Europe is feeling cost pressure, while its platform now reaches European buyers through a second Chinese group.

Leapmotor, Stellantis's Chinese partner, reported unaudited third-quarter revenue of 31.7 billion yuan, up 63 percent, on a record 310,052 deliveries. Net profit fell 73 percent to 40 million yuan and gross margin dropped to 10.9 percent from 14.5 percent a year earlier, which the company blames on raw-material costs, product mix and higher shipping costs. Exports rose to 63,278 cars, about a fifth of volume.

On the same day, FAW's Hongqi launched the EHS4 electric SUV in Europe, built on Leapmotor's LEAP 3.5 platform. Italy is the first market, with introductory prices from 32,900 euros. It has a 160 kW rear motor and a 68.7 kWh LFP battery integrated into the chassis, giving up to 460 km WLTP and charging at up to 178 kW on a 400 V system. Hongqi plans ten models for Europe next year.

Platform sharing between Chinese groups is now reaching Europe at Volkswagen ID.3 prices, while thinner margins suggest the price war has limits.
