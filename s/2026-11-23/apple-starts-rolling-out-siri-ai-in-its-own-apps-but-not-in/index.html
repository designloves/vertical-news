Apple starts rolling out Siri AI in its own apps, but not in the EU

Siri app skills could become a new interface for your product, while European users will have to wait.

Apple has begun rolling out new Siri AI features in eight of its own apps: Calendar, Files, Mail, Maps, Messages, Photos, Safari and Weather. A developer preview of the App Intents API lets third-party apps expose content and actions, such as checking a delivery or booking a table, and build 'app skills' that guide Siri through several steps and across apps.

Users must name a background app, but not one that is in the foreground, showing a Live Activity or already part of the conversation. For now, preview integrations work only in Xcode builds and internal TestFlight, with App Store submissions expected in spring. Siri AI is not available in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS while Apple talks to the European Commission. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports that recent Siri layoffs hit the old infrastructure team, and he does not expect a more advanced agent before late 2027.

UX teams should start mapping their key tasks to intents and think about how they should sound in a voice flow, since Siri will now invoke them by context.
