Lovable adds GPT-6.2 models, new payment connectors and publishing to a company email domain

Prompt-to-app tools are moving from prototypes to paid and internally deployed apps, which changes how design and product teams hand off and govern work.

Swedish prompt-to-app builder Lovable updated its platform on Friday. Apps built with it can now call OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra for complex reasoning and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday tasks, both with text and image input. New connectors for Square and for Paystack, which covers Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and Côte d'Ivoire, follow Adyen and Airwallex connectors added the day before.

Enterprise customers can now publish an app to a verified company email domain, so anyone with that address can open it after logging in. That turns Lovable into a way to ship internal tools without a separate hosting and access setup. Paid web plans also get a free domain for the first year.

The direction is clear: payments, access control and model choice are being built in, so what starts as a prototype can run as a real product. For design and product teams, that raises questions about who reviews and maintains these apps once they go live, especially after recent reports of Lovable-built apps exposing data.
