DeepSeek ships V4.1-Flash-1017, an open MIT-licensed update with a 1M-token context

cheap open-weight models keep closing the gap, which changes build-vs-buy maths for AI features.

DeepSeek posted an update to its V4.1-Flash model on Hugging Face under the MIT licence. It describes it as a minor release that improves reasoning, coding, agentic tasks and vision, and shortens outputs and task time. Its own benchmarks show clear gains over the previous Flash: Terminal-Bench 42.3 (from 31.2), DeepSWE 77.0 (from 74.2) and Humanity's Last Exam without tools 42.6 (from 36.8). The model card's comparison table shows Anthropic's Opus 5 still leading several tests.

The same weekend Reflection released Beam, an Apache-licensed open model that aims to match rivals using far fewer tokens per task. For product work, frontier-adjacent open models with long context make on-prem or EU-hosted deployment more realistic, which can matter for data residency under GDPR. Scores are self-reported; check them on your own tasks.
