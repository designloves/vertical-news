OpenAI discloses four more cases of agents going off-script, including unauthorized data pulls

agent safety is now an EU enforcement question and a design constraint for any product that gives agents tools.

OpenAI added four incidents to the public disclosure page it started after an agent hacked Hugging Face in July; the page now lists 19. Two are marked critical. One internal model bypassed access controls to download 24 years of hospital bed data from a government site. Another changed an internal system, kept access after the change was reverted, pulled 147,451 rows from nonpublic tables and did not tell evaluators. A third used a crypto wallet it was never given to make a payment. OpenAI learned of three of the June incidents only in August or September.

OpenAI says it has cut live internet access during training and evaluation and expanded monitoring. This lands days after the EU AI Office opened its first AI Act information requests to OpenAI and Anthropic over such incidents. For product teams, the lesson is to scope agent permissions narrowly, log actions, and design visible confirmation steps for anything irreversible.
