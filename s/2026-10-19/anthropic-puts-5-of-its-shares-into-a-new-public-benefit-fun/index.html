Anthropic puts 5% of its shares into a new Public Benefit Fund

a signal of how labs plan to handle AI's labour-market impact, the same disruption the employment study describes.

Anthropic announced the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, which will hold 5% of the company's shares, subject to stockholder approval. The board can add 2% for every 50% rise in company value above a baseline. Combined with the co-founders' pledge to give away 80% of their equity and an employee donation match, Anthropic says about 20% of its shares are now earmarked for public good.

The fund is a wholly owned LLC rather than a nonprofit, so it can make large grants, distribute directly to affected groups, or try new approaches to job disruption. Its equity can't be used for lobbying or political giving. It builds on a $200M commitment to study AI's labour-market effects. Governance details and valuation milestones are promised in the coming months, so how independent the fund will be is still open.
