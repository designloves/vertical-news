AI infrastructure wobbles: Nscale's IPO valuation cut, chip stocks fall eight days running

compute pricing and availability for AI features depend on how this neocloud shakeout plays out.

Bloomberg reports that investors are pushing UK AI cloud provider Nscale toward an IPO valuation of about $16–19B, well below its $30B-plus target. The report cites a Norwegian site running at a fraction of planned capacity and Microsoft leaving a UK anchor deal. On Friday the Philadelphia semiconductor index fell for an eighth straight session, led by memory makers, and CoreWeave dropped about 12%.

The Information separately reports executives expect only a handful of the hundreds of "neoclouds" to survive. Meanwhile power deals continue: the US DOE advanced $4.4B in loans to restart three nuclear plants that will partly serve AI data centres. This is market noise rather than a product change, but European teams buying GPU capacity should watch provider stability and contract terms.
