Altman confirms OpenAI is building a humanoid robot, with a demo targeted for 2027

OpenAI is entering physical AI, the area where vehicle and machinery OEMs compete for talent and partners.

Sam Altman said on a podcast that OpenAI will build a humanoid robot, arguing that a human shape fits a world built for people. In a Fortune interview he said he expects a demo in 2027 and robots on public streets only several years after that. He framed the control software, the robot's "brain," as the hard part and the body as secondary.

OpenAI lists 27 robotics roles covering motion control, actuator design and manufacturing. The near-term aim is general-purpose robots that help skilled workers build infrastructure. There is no product, price or design yet, and Tesla and XPENG are further along. For engineering readers, the interesting part is the hiring mix: actuators and manufacturing point to OpenAI building hardware capability, not only licensing models to robot makers.
