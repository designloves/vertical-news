ElevenLabs is reported to be in early talks to raise money at a 33 billion dollar valuation

Investors are still paying high prices for AI voice companies whose revenue is growing fast.

Bloomberg reports that ElevenLabs is in early talks to raise money at a 33 billion dollar valuation, up from 22 billion in its last round. The company is aiming to close in the first quarter of 2027, and the deal may include a sale of existing shares. The size of the round is not known. Annualised revenue is reported at about 700 million dollars, up 100 million since September.

ElevenLabs recently released its Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo voice models, the latter with 150-millisecond latency, and bought Japan's Kotoba earlier this month. Its chief executive has said IPO preparations will start in 2028. The company has not confirmed the talks.

Read together with DeepL's launch, the funding shows voice becoming a main way to use software, not an add-on. Product teams adding voice agents have more capable vendors to choose from, and those vendors have strong pricing power.
