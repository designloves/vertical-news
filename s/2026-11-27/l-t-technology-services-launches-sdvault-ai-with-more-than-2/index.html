L&T Technology Services launches SDVault.ai, with more than 250 agents for software-defined vehicle engineering

Engineering service providers are packaging AI agents across the whole V-model, from requirements to virtual ECU validation.

Indian engineering services firm L&T Technology Services has launched SDVault.ai, a platform for OEMs and suppliers that develop software-defined vehicles. It combines more than 250 specialised AI agents with rule-based automation for tasks that need repeatable, traceable results.

The platform links 12 engineering disciplines, including E/E architecture, functional safety, embedded debugging, cybersecurity, verification, release assurance and virtual ECU validation. LTTS says early pilots let teams take on 30 to 40% more work, and cut some ticket triage from about four hours to ten minutes. These are vendor figures from selected pilots. They are not an independent measure of overall development time, and no customers are named.

The mix of AI agents and deterministic automation fits regulated work under ISO 26262 and UN R155. There, agents can draft and triage, but traceable tools must produce the evidence for type approval.
