A second main contractor leaves Agratas' UK gigafactory, which now aims to open at the end of 2027

Delays at Europe's battery plants affect cell supply for JLR and other OEMs that plan local sourcing under rules-of-origin requirements.

TSL, the contractor building the exterior of Tata-owned Agratas' battery cell plant near Bridgwater in Somerset, is reported to be leaving once its work is finished. The Guardian reported that the two sides failed to agree terms. TSL replaced Sir Robert McAlpine in June, so this is the second main contractor change in about six months.

Agratas says it is on track to meet customer requirements and will not replace TSL. NG Bailey is already doing the internal fit-out. The plant was first meant to open in 2026 and is now confirmed for the end of 2027. It expects about 1,500 operational jobs in 2027 and around 4,600 at full output.

Agratas is part of the Tata Group, which owns Jaguar Land Rover. The UK's largest planned gigafactory is central to building EVs in Britain that meet EU–UK trade rules on battery content.
