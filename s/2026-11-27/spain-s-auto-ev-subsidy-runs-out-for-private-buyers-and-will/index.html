Spain's Auto+ EV subsidy runs out for private buyers and will not be extended

Another European EV incentive ends early, which makes demand harder to forecast for 2027.

Spain's Auto+ purchase subsidy for electric cars ran out of money for private buyers on 19 November. A waiting list now covers applications that are withdrawn or rejected. The 2026 budget was about 400 million euros: 350 million for private buyers and 50 million for businesses and the self-employed.

By mid-November, about 103,000 applications averaging 3,167 euros had used 93% of the private budget, and 86% of the business budget had gone. An Industry Ministry official ruled out an extension, citing legal and budget limits and the coming parliamentary elections. Auto+ started in July, applied back to 1 January, and replaced the regionally run Moves III scheme.

Spain is a major production country for Stellantis, Renault and Volkswagen Group. An early end to the incentive could weaken local EV demand going into 2027, just as automakers work toward the EU's averaged CO2 targets.
