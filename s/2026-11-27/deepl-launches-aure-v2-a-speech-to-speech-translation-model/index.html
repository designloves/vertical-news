DeepL launches Aure v2, a speech-to-speech translation model, and reports fast growth in voice use

Live voice translation is quickly becoming a standard feature in meeting and service products.

DeepL launched Aure v2 at its first Unmute Summit in Tokyo, just 15 days after the first version. Aure translates speech directly into speech, without a text step in between. DeepL says the median delay is half what it was. In its own benchmark of one-minute real recordings across ten language pairs, DeepL reports 32% fewer English–Japanese errors than Google's Gemini 3.5 Live Translate, while noting that real-world results may differ.

Aure v2 launches in English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese and Korean. It is available in DeepL Voice for meetings, DeepL Voice for in-person conversations, and the Voice API. DeepL says voice minutes grew more than eightfold year on year in the third quarter, and active meeting subscriptions grew about ninefold in Germany.

For product teams, an API with this latency makes live multilingual support, field service calls and supplier meetings realistic features to build. The benchmark figures are DeepL's own.
