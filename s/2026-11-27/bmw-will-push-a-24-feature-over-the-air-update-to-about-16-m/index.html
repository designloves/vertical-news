BMW will push a 24-feature over-the-air update to about 16 million cars in December

It shows how far a large OEM's software-defined fleet now reaches, including cars on older operating systems.

BMW's software version 11/2026.3 goes to about 16 million vehicles in 88 countries from December. It has five new functions and 19 improved ones. Cars on BMW Operating System X get all 24. Cars on OS 8.5 and OS 9 get nine. Installation takes about 20 minutes.

In Germany, Highway Assistant now works on more roads that have physically separated lanes. In the US, the Highway & Commuting Assistant now works in city traffic too: it can stop at red lights and pull away on green. Other additions include better forecasts of how busy public chargers will be, Google Maps Places data in destination search, a driving-style analysis for the iX3, and a more capable AI voice assistant in France and Spain.

Shipping ADAS changes over the air to a fleet this size depends on regression testing and simulation-based sign-off across many hardware and software combinations, which is the kind of virtual validation workload many OEMs are now scaling up.
