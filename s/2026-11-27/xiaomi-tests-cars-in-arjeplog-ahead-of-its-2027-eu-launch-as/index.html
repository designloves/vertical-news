Xiaomi tests cars in Arjeplog ahead of its 2027 EU launch as its car losses grow

Another Chinese EV maker is coming to Europe and validating its cars at Swedish winter test sites.

Xiaomi is testing its cars at seven sites in Sweden, Germany, Spain and Italy ahead of a European launch, including the Arjeplog winter test area and the Nürburgring. Germany will be its first market. Xiaomi has letters of intent with eight German dealer groups, including Emil Frey, and plans to exhibit at IAA Mobility in September 2027. Cars built in China face a 45.3% EU countervailing duty.

The car division's third quarter was weaker. Its operating loss rose to 3.4 billion yuan, from 2.6 billion in the second quarter. Deliveries fell 7.2% to 100,975, and gross margin dropped to 19.2% from 25.5%. Xiaomi has delivered about 336,000 cars in ten months, against a full-year target of 550,000.

Arjeplog's test service providers and engineering consultancies get another Chinese client, and European OEMs get a competitor whose cars are built around its own software stack.
