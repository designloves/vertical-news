OpenAI fixes a billing error, and GPT-6.2 Astra limits now run out 40% faster

A pricing bug that quietly gave users more usage shows how hard it is to meter agent-heavy products, and why limits need to be visible to users.

OpenAI Codex engineer Thibault Sottiaux said a billing mistake in the GPT-6.2 rollout gave most users the wrong discount on GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. Now that it is fixed, usage limits run out about 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than last week. They still last longer than with the previous GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol. Other models were not affected.

OpenAI will not charge users for the difference. To compensate, Go, Plus, Pro and Business subscribers get one extra saved reset, and Business users get Fast Mode free until the end of the year.

The fix comes as OpenAI starts merging Chat and Work onto one shared usage meter. For teams pricing their own AI features, it is a reminder that limits measured in tokens are hard for users to predict. Clear meters and compensation policies matter as much as the price itself.
