IBM and Microsoft launch Imagine Enterprise with 6,000 ready-made industry agents

Big vendors are packaging governed, industry-specific agents, with manufacturing and mobility on the roadmap.

IBM and Microsoft announced Imagine Enterprise, a platform for building, running and governing AI agents for industry-specific processes, aimed especially at regulated sectors. IBM Consulting contributes more than 6,000 pre-built agents and assets. Microsoft supplies the base: Foundry, Copilot Studio, Fabric, Azure, and its Entra, Purview and Defender identity and security tools.

The first industry frameworks cover financial services, insurance and retail. The companies plan to extend the same blueprint to manufacturing, energy, the public sector and mobility. Clients can have IBM Consulting run the platform, build with it jointly, or manage it themselves, and forward-deployed engineers from both companies are available for qualifying projects. Financial firm SEI is the only named customer so far.

The pitch is governance: controls, observability and compliance support built in from the start. For automotive and industrial companies, the planned mobility and manufacturing versions are the ones to watch, since agent rollouts there tend to stall on audit and safety requirements rather than on model capability.
