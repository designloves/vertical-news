Anthropic adds Claude Sheets, a meeting agent and shared whiteboards

AI labs are moving into everyday office software, competing directly with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.

At an event in San Francisco, Anthropic introduced three collaboration products. Claude Sheets, in beta for subscribers on web, desktop and mobile, lets Claude build and edit spreadsheets with multiple tabs, formulas and formatting. Anyone with edit access can change the file, Claude's edits are flagged, and files export to Excel.

Claude Meetings joins video calls, starting with Google Meet, and takes notes. It announces itself in the meeting chat, and it will not join calls with outside guests unless an admin allows it. Afterwards, users can ask Claude about decisions and action items. It is limited to Team and Enterprise plans. Claude Whiteboards, in beta for all paid users, groups brainstorming input by theme and drafts action items for each person.

The launch came days before Anthropic's planned stock market listing. For product teams, the design choices are worth noting: visible attribution of AI edits, clear disclosure when an agent is present, and admin control over external meetings are becoming standard patterns for AI in shared workspaces.
