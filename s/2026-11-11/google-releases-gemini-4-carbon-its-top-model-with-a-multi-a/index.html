Google releases Gemini 4 Carbon, its top model, with a multi-agent 'swarm' mode

A new top-tier model at a low launch price shifts cost and capability choices for coding, agent and research work.

Google has made Gemini 4 Carbon available to paying API customers through Google AI Studio and Gemini for business, and is rolling it out to AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search and its Antigravity coding environment. Google describes Carbon as its strongest model for hard, long-running agent tasks and cybersecurity. It sits above Gemini 4 Argon, which Google still recommends for most software and knowledge work because Carbon spends more tokens reasoning and checking its own output.

Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, the same as Argon. From 30 November the public price doubles to $4 and $20. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Zapier's AutomationBench and Perplexity's WANDR at less than half the cost per task of the next-best outside model; these are vendor-reported figures.

A new /swarm preview in Antigravity turns one prompt into a coordinated team of agents: Carbon plans and audits while faster models work in parallel and separate verifier agents test the changes. Google says resistance to prompt injection still needs work, which matters for anyone wiring the model into tools that read untrusted content.
