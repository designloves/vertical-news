SKF sets the record date for its Vertevo spin-off and gets the listing prospectus approved

Europe's largest bearing maker is days away from splitting its automotive business into a separately listed company.

SKF's board has set 27 November as the record date for handing shares in SKF Vertevo, its automotive bearings and seals business, to SKF shareholders. Holders will receive one Vertevo share for every five SKF shares of the same series. SKF shares trade with the right to Vertevo shares until 25 November and without it from 26 November. Trading in Vertevo on Nasdaq Stockholm is expected to start on 1 December under the tickers SKFVER A and SKFVER B.

Sweden's financial regulator, Finansinspektionen, approved the listing prospectus on 10 November, and Nasdaq Stockholm has admitted the shares to trading subject to customary conditions. Fractions of shares will be pooled and sold, with the proceeds paid to holders. Shareholders approved the distribution at an extraordinary meeting on 1 October.

The separation started in September 2024. It will leave SKF focused on industrial bearings and create a stand-alone supplier of automotive bearings, including e-mobility and EV products. Vertevo's Capital Markets Day in Stockholm on 17 November should provide its first detailed targets.
