Product manager openings reach a four-year high as design and front-end roles shrink

Hard data on where hiring demand is moving in product, design and engineering as AI tools spread.

Lenny Rachitsky's late-2026 job market report, based on TrueUp postings across more than 9,000 tech companies and US hiring data from Live Data Technologies, finds open product manager roles at their highest in nearly four years, up 75% from the March 2023 low. PMs' share of hiring has risen from 16% in 2021 to 21%, the only core role to gain share.

Design looks much weaker. There are about 1,900 open product design roles worldwide, the lowest since tracking began and down 28% in a year. There are now 3.8 open PM roles for every design role, up from 1.9 in early 2023, and US user-research hiring is down 66% from 2022. Engineering openings grew only 2.5% in the past year; mobile and front-end demand has nearly halved, while openings for forward-deployed engineers have grown 4.6 times to about 1,500.

AI skills now shape job ads. 'LLM' is the most-mentioned skill in PM postings, and Claude has become the second most-mentioned skill in designer listings after Figma. For design and front-end professionals, the message is to pair craft with hands-on AI tool fluency.
