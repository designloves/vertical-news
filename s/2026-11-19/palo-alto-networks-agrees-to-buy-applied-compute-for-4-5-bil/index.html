Palo Alto Networks agrees to buy Applied Compute for $4.5 billion in stock

Security vendors are betting that companies will fine-tune and run their own open-weight models, and need to secure them.

Palo Alto Networks signed a definitive agreement to acquire Applied Compute in an all-stock deal worth about $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments. The deal is expected to close in Palo Alto Networks' fiscal third quarter of 2027, pending regulatory approval. Applied Compute runs a platform for post-training and inference that lets companies adapt open-weight models to their own data and run them, and it will keep serving its customers.

Applied Compute's chief executive and co-founder Yash Patil will become a co-chief AI officer at Palo Alto Networks. According to AI Weekly, citing the Wall Street Journal, the start-up was founded in 2025 by three former OpenAI staff and was valued at $3.25 billion when it raised $350 million at the start of November.

The deal shows that customised, in-house models are becoming a security and governance question. Teams that fine-tune open models on company data should expect more tooling, and more scrutiny, around that work.
