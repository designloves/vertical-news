Boston Dynamics' Atlas moves beyond car plants to GlobalFoundries chip fabs, including Dresden

Humanoid robots tested in Hyundai factories are being tried in other industries, and inspection robots are already doing large volumes of work.

Chipmaker GlobalFoundries has joined Boston Dynamics' early adopter programme for the Atlas humanoid. It is the first customer outside the car industry and the first chipmaker to test the robot. Atlas will start work at key GlobalFoundries sites early next year, first moving and sorting parts. The two companies will then work out which tasks in chip production a humanoid can take on. The number of robots has not been disclosed.

GlobalFoundries is also expanding its use of Boston Dynamics' four-legged Spot robot from Vermont and Singapore to sites including New York and Dresden. In Singapore, two Spot units have travelled about 900 km through the equipment areas under the cleanroom since April and carried out more than 90,000 inspections with 99% mission success. The company says this supports its move to predictive maintenance.

Until now, Atlas development has focused on Hyundai's car plants. The Spot numbers show that automated inspection rounds, such as thermal and gauge checks, are delivering results today, while humanoids are still at the pilot stage.
