Google lets Gemini users opt out of training without losing history, but keeps chats about two months even with history off

Privacy controls and how long data is kept are now part of the product, and they matter to teams putting work into AI assistants.

Google has split two Gemini settings. Users can now keep their chat history while opting out of having their conversations used to train models. Until now, turning off training also meant losing history and some connected apps.

Google's updated privacy notice also says that even with Gemini History turned off, future chats will be kept for around two months, up from 72 hours. Google says the data is used to respond to users, process feedback and for safety, and human reviewers may see it. The setting text still says 72 hours, and Google has not given a start date. For comparison, OpenAI keeps temporary chats for up to 30 days and Anthropic keeps Claude conversations for 30 days by default.

For UX teams, this is a useful example of separating controls that users see as different choices. For anyone pasting design files, code or test data into consumer assistants, the longer retention is a reason to check company policy and use business accounts, where admins control retention.
