OpenAI says its models found up to 14% gains in power, performance and area on AMD GPU designs

It is a concrete example of AI improving mature engineering designs that experts had already optimised.

OpenAI reported that its models rewrote SystemVerilog code for selected blocks of AMD's flagship GPU designs and found up to 14% improvement in power, performance and area on circuits AMD had already tuned for years. Synopsys tools checked that each rewrite behaved the same as the original and measured the trade-offs. AMD separately reported single-digit gains on a next-generation CPU.

OpenAI also said that on its own first inference chip, called Jalapeño, AI-assisted design cut area by about 13% in the vector unit and 10% in the matrix unit in silicon, with nine months from design start to tape-out. OpenAI admits its models sometimes dropped features that were in the original designs. The AMD figures come from OpenAI's own reporting.

For engineering teams, the pattern matters more than the chip: AI proposes changes, and established verification tools prove them equivalent. The same split between generation and formal checking is relevant wherever simulation and sign-off tools already exist.
