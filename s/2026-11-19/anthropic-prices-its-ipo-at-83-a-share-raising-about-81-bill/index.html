Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, raising about $81 billion at a valuation near $1.5 trillion

The maker of Claude now has public-market money and public-market scrutiny, which will shape its pricing, roadmap and enterprise deals.

Anthropic priced its initial public offering on Wednesday evening at $83 per share, above the $70–78 range it set on 10 November. It also increased the deal from 825 million to about 980 million shares, raising roughly $81.3 billion. CNBC puts the fully diluted market value at around $1.5 trillion. The shares are due to start trading on Nasdaq today under the ticker ANT. Google, Microsoft, Nvidia and AMD have also agreed to invest a combined $26 billion at the IPO price in a parallel private placement.

The prospectus shows revenue of $33.2 billion for the nine months to September, about twelve times the year before, and an annualised run rate of about $81 billion at the end of October. The reported net loss of $104 billion comes mostly from accounting changes on convertible notes and derivatives, not from operations. Amazon and Google will together own nearly 30% after the listing.

For teams building on Claude, the listing adds stability and a capital base comparable to OpenAI's. It also means quarterly reporting, so pricing changes and margin pressure on API and seat products will be easier to see.
