Hongqi looks for a European factory and names Stellantis and VW as possible partners

More Chinese brands want to build in Europe, which affects plant use, platform sharing and local sourcing for suppliers.

FAW's international chief Xiao Xiao told Reuters at the European launch of the Hongqi EHS4 in Venice that building cars in Europe is a strategic priority. He said Stellantis is not the only possible partner and pointed to FAW's long relationship with Volkswagen in China. Volkswagen Group China said there are no talks with FAW about building cars in Europe.

Reuters had earlier reported talks about a Stellantis plant in Spain, where Stellantis already builds cars with Leapmotor. Hongqi prefers to use existing European plants rather than build its own. The EHS4 uses a Leapmotor platform and starts at €32,900 as a battery-electric car, with a range-extender version due in 2027. Hongqi sells in 27 European markets through 90 dealers and plans 10 models in Europe in 2027.

For European suppliers, local production by Chinese brands means new sourcing decisions, and more pressure on under-used European plants to find partners.
