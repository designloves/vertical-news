Boston Dynamics expands Spot inspections at GlobalFoundries, which will test the Atlas humanoid from 2027

Inspection robots in chip fabs show what autonomous condition monitoring can do in a demanding production setting.

GlobalFoundries will use Boston Dynamics' Spot robot for autonomous asset inspection at its fabs in Malta, New York, Burlington, Vermont, Dresden and Singapore. Spot has been running in Burlington for more than four years. In Singapore, two robots have covered nearly 900 km since April and completed more than 90,000 sub-fab inspections with a 99% mission success rate.

GlobalFoundries moved 875 routine inspections to Spot in two weeks and plans to more than double that. It will also be the first customer outside the car industry to test the Atlas humanoid, through an early-adopter programme, with on-site testing from early 2027.

For factories and test labs, the numbers are a useful benchmark for robotic inspection rounds that collect thermal, acoustic and visual data, and for the shift from reactive to predictive maintenance.
