Anthropic prices its IPO at a reported $83 a share, valuing it at about $1.5 trillion

The first frontier AI lab to go public sets a market price on the models many product teams now build on.

Anthropic priced its initial public offering on Wednesday evening at $83 a share, according to TechCrunch, above the $70–78 range it had marketed to investors. At that price the company is valued at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, up from $965 billion at its last private round in May. CNBC reported that the offering raised more than $81 billion, making it the largest IPO on record. Shares start trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT today.

Alongside the IPO, a group of strategic investors including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting shares in a private placement. Amazon owns nearly 15% of the company and Google almost 14%. According to the S-1, each of the seven co-founders sold just under 3.4 million shares, worth about $282 million each.

For teams that depend on Claude, a listed Anthropic will publish quarterly results, giving more visibility into pricing, capacity and priorities. The pricing came despite a nervous week for AI stocks after Nvidia's leadership change.
