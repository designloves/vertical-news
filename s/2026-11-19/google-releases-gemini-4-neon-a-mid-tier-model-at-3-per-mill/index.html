Google releases Gemini 4 Neon, a mid-tier model at $3 per million input tokens

A strong coding model at a middle price gives frontend and product teams another option to test on cost per task.

Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Neon, which sits between Gemini 4 Carbon, its model for the hardest long-running work, and Hydrogen, the fast everyday model launched last week. Neon has a one-million-token context window and four thinking levels. API pricing is $3 per million input tokens, $15 per million output tokens and $0.15 per million cached input tokens. Google says a typical task costs 32% less than with Carbon at the default setting.

Google's own benchmark figures put Neon first on Artificial Analysis's coding agent index and second on the Vals Index, with 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1. It is available in the paid Gemini API, Gemini for business, Antigravity and the Gemini app for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Vercel added it to its AI Gateway on the same day, with tool calling and structured outputs.

With GPT-6.2 Sol, Gemini 4 Hydrogen and now Neon all released within a week, teams have several mid-priced models to compare on their own tasks. Google has not said whether there are any regional limits in Europe.
