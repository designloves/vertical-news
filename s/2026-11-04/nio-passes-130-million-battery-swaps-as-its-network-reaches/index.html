Nio passes 130 million battery swaps as its network reaches 4,236 stations

Battery swapping is still a serious alternative to fast charging at scale, and it puts different demands on vehicle and pack design.

Nio said it completed its 130 millionth battery swap on 3 November. The previous 10 million took 88 days, since 120 million was passed on 7 August, which works out to more than 110,000 swaps a day. The company's China swap network grew by 230 stations over that period to 4,236.

Nio also reports 1,077 highway swap stations linking 550 cities, and says 84.5% of users now have a swap station within 3 km of home or work. Its fifth-generation stations, launched on 7 August, can serve Nio, Onvo and Firefly vehicles from the same platform, as they support different wheelbases. All figures are the company's own.

For European engineers, swapping is a reminder that pack mounting, connectors and standardised dimensions are design choices with big operational effects. Real-world use at this scale also gives Nio a large body of durability data on repeated pack handling.
