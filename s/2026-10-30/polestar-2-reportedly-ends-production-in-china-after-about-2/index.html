Polestar 2 reportedly ends production in China after about 200,000 cars

The brand now has a gap in its lineup while it deals with a US exit and a heavy reliance on Europe.

Reports say the last Polestar 2 has left Geely's Luqiao plant in China, bringing total output to just over 200,000 cars since production began in March 2020. Polestar had not issued an official statement or final production figure at the time of the reports, so the number should be treated as unofficial. A completely new successor is due in early 2027, which leaves a gap of several months with no new Polestar 2 to build.

The timing is awkward. Polestar is leaving the US market after the Commerce Department's export-control bureau declined to authorise it under the Connected Vehicle Rule, even though Volvo Cars, also controlled by Geely, received approval in May. Polestar says it will not appeal and will focus on Europe, which accounts for nearly 80% of its retail volume. Automotive News also reports the company is widening its range with the Polestar 4 SUV and further models despite continued losses.

For the wider industry, the case shows how ownership and software origin can now decide market access in the US, an issue for any European brand with Chinese-linked software or hardware in its supply chain.
