OpenAI's quarterly revenue reportedly passes $10 billion for the first time

Revenue growth driven by enterprise and agent products shows where the money in AI is moving, and supports the case for tighter pricing.

Bloomberg reports, citing unnamed sources, that OpenAI made about $10 billion in revenue in the third quarter, nearly 50% more than in the second quarter. Growth between the first and second quarters was about 18%. The adjusted operating margin improved from -150% to -109%, and the annual run rate is put at $50 billion. OpenAI says it has more than one billion users, most of them on free or cheaper plans.

The reported reasons are demand for newer models, fewer discounts, and more focus on enterprise products and AI agents. For comparison, Anthropic's IPO filing shows $17 billion in third-quarter revenue, though the two companies count partner sales differently, so the figures are not directly comparable.

For product teams, the message is that AI vendors are moving from growth at any price towards margin. Expect fewer discounts and more pricing tied to agent use and business seats.
