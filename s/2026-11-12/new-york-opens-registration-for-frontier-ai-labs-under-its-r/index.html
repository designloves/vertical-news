New York opens registration for frontier AI labs under its RAISE Act

A second large US jurisdiction now requires published safety frameworks and 72-hour incident reports, similar in spirit to EU AI Act duties for general-purpose models.

New York has opened a registration portal for frontier AI developers under the RAISE Act, which takes effect at the start of 2027. Companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue, including OpenAI and Anthropic, must register by 1 January. They must publish their safety frameworks, issue model transparency reports and report critical safety incidents within 72 hours.

A new state office, DIGIT, will enforce the law, with fines of up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for later ones. A new state-backed centre at Binghamton University will independently test advanced AI systems and help review the labs' safety frameworks.

For European teams, the requirements look familiar from the EU's code of practice for general-purpose AI. Large providers are converging on one set of safety documents that can satisfy several regulators.
