Anthropic sets IPO terms that value it at up to $1.4 trillion

The biggest AI listing so far will make Anthropic's finances, priorities and governance public, which matters for anyone building on Claude.

Anthropic has set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares, raising about $58–64 billion. At the top of the range this implies a fully diluted valuation of $1.404 trillion, up from $965 billion in May. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC will also buy $26 billion of non-voting shares at the IPO price, with Google and Nvidia putting in $10 billion each. The stock is to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT from around 19 November, and an Australian retail offer opened on 11 November.

The filing shows run-rate revenue of $81 billion at the end of October, third-quarter revenue of $17 billion and a nine-month net loss of $104 billion. The co-founders keep a share class with 50.1% of the votes, and the Long-Term Benefit Trust will elect most of the board.

For customers, a listed Anthropic will report its numbers every quarter, giving clearer signals on pricing, capacity and product focus.
