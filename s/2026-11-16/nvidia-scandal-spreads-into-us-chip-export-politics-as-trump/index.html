Nvidia scandal spreads into US chip-export politics as Trump heads to China

Any tightening of US export policy after this week would affect the supply and price of AI hardware worldwide.

The fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia chief executive has reached Washington. Semafor reports that Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is under pressure over claims that he slowed restrictions on AI chip exports to China and left loopholes that let advanced chips reach the country indirectly. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says his department pushed for months to blacklist Megaspeed, a Singapore cloud company the US is investigating for alleged chip smuggling, but that decision belongs to Commerce.

Megaspeed told CNBC that Alice Huang, the executive at the centre of the reports, left in August 2025, and that it has hired outside counsel for an internal review. Lutnick is still due to travel with President Trump to meet Xi Jinping this week, and the White House says it has full confidence in him.

Nvidia reports quarterly results on Tuesday, its first under interim chief executive Colette Kress. Investors will watch for any guidance on China sales and on who will lead the company.
