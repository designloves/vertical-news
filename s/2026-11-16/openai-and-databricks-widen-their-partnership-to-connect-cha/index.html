OpenAI and Databricks widen their partnership to connect ChatGPT and Codex to governed company data

Enterprise AI is settling into a pairing of model provider and governed data platform, with spending controls per team becoming standard.

Databricks and OpenAI have extended the multi-year deal they signed in September 2025. OpenAI's GPT models, including the GPT-6 family and the open-weight gpt-oss, can now be called directly from SQL, notebooks and agents on Databricks. There are new plugins that bring Databricks' Genie analytics and a data-analyst tool into ChatGPT, and Codex gets a gateway tool, agent skills and a data-science plugin. A template for OpenAI's Agents SDK is available in Databricks Apps.

Through its Unity Gateway, engineering leaders can set budgets and track AI spending by team. No prices were given. Each company is also a customer of the other: Databricks says OpenAI is one of its largest customers, and Databricks engineers are getting Codex along with early access to new models.

For product leaders, the message is that the hard part of enterprise AI is now access to permissioned data and cost control, not the choice of model. Features that work inside a customer's existing data governance are easier to sell.
