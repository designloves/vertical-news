Deutsche Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud will train microagi's robot skills from simulated factory work

Sim-to-real robot training on sovereign German infrastructure targets European manufacturers wary of sending production data abroad.

Munich robotics AI company microagi will train its industrial robot models on Deutsche Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud, run by T-Systems at its Munich AI factory. Videos of real workflows are turned into simulations where robots practise tasks, and the learned skill is then moved to the physical robot. The first targets are manufacturing and logistics.

Telekom stresses that production data stays in Germany under the customer's control. No financial terms were disclosed.

For manufacturing and test engineers, this is another sign that simulation is becoming the training ground for factory automation, not only for product design. Data sovereignty is being used as a selling point against US cloud providers. The open question is how well skills learned in simulation hold up on real lines, where parts, lighting and tolerances vary more than in a virtual cell.
