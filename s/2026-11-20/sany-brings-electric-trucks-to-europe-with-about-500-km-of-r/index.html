Sany brings electric trucks to Europe with about 500 km of range, priced 20% below European makers

Chinese electric heavy trucks are now competing directly with Volvo, Scania, Daimler and MAN on price.

Chinese machinery group Sany is pushing its electric trucks into Europe. Its main product is the e263 electric tractor, with a usable battery capacity of 620 kWh and a quoted range of about 500 km. Sany is focusing on depot charging for now, and on construction work such as tippers and cement mixers, with logistics to follow. The first customers have used the trucks since May, and the normal lead time is about four months.

Sany says it will price its trucks about 20% below European manufacturers. For service it works with Alltrucks, which has about 650 workshops, and uses the dealer network, warranty processes and spare-parts system of Putzmeister, the German concrete-pump maker it owns. Sany deployed about 22,000 electric tractors in China in 2025, but has not given sales targets for Europe.

This lands as the EU reviews its CO2 rules for trucks and as European truck makers ask for faster support for electric trucks. Lower-priced Chinese trucks add pressure on their margins and on suppliers of drivetrains and bearings.
