Anthropic opens 34% above its IPO price and closes its first day valued at about $1.8 trillion

Investors now put a public price on a frontier AI lab, and that price shapes funding, pricing and competition across the AI tools teams rely on.

Anthropic's shares began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday. They opened at $111.50, about 34% above the $83 offer price, which briefly gave the company a market value of roughly $2 trillion. The stock lost some of that gain during the day and closed at $97.17, up about 17%. CNBC put the fully diluted value at the close at about $1.8 trillion, making it the seventh most valuable US company. More than 846 million shares changed hands, compared with 522 million on SpaceX's first day in June.

The company sold 980 million shares and raised $81.3 billion, the largest US IPO on record. Its filings show $33.2 billion in revenue for the first nine months of 2026, up about twelvefold. The same period showed a net loss of $104 billion, mostly from changes in the value of convertible notes and similar instruments. Amazon owns 14.7% and Google 13.7%, and the seven co-founders keep a majority of voting power.

For product and engineering teams, the main effect is visibility. As a listed company, Anthropic will report results every quarter, so pricing, capacity and model plans will face more public scrutiny.
