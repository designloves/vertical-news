Analysts weigh Schaeffler's China exposure and SKF's value ahead of the Vertevo listing

The two European bearing majors face different tests: China car demand for Schaeffler, and the automotive spin-off for SKF.

UBS kept Schaeffler at Neutral with a price target of 8.10 euros. Analyst David Lesne pointed to China, where car retail sales fell about 25% year on year in the first half of November, with wholesale volumes down almost as much and no sign of recovery. For a supplier with large Chinese automotive volumes, that weakness weighs on bearing and drivetrain orders.

Pareto kept its Buy rating on SKF with a target of 315 kronor ahead of the Vertevo listing on 1 December. It values SKF and Vertevo together at about 320 kronor per SKF share, with Vertevo worth about 8 billion kronor. Pareto expects Vertevo's margin to be about 6% in 2028, with 1.5 billion kronor of investment in 2027 and about 500 million kronor a year of restructuring costs from 2027 to 2029.

For engineers at OEMs and suppliers, Vertevo's restructuring plan and Schaeffler's China exposure will both shape how hard these companies push on price and capacity in the next sourcing rounds.
