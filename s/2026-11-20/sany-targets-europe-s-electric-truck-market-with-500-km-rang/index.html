Sany targets Europe's electric truck market with 500 km range and prices 20% below local makers

Chinese heavy trucks are coming for the construction and depot segments where Volvo, Scania and Daimler earn good margins.

In an interview at IAA Transportation, Sany eTrucks Europe set out its plans. Its flagship e263 tractor has a 620 kWh usable battery and about 500 km of range. The first trucks have been with customers since May 2026, starting with construction uses such as tippers and cement mixers before moving into logistics. Lead times are about four months.

Sany says it aims to price about 20% below European makers. Service runs through the Alltrucks network of about 650 workshops and through Putzmeister, the German concrete pump maker Sany owns. Sany sold about 22,000 electric tractor units in China in 2025 but gave no European sales target.

For European truck makers and their suppliers, this adds price pressure in exactly the segments where electric trucks are taking off first: short, predictable routes with depot charging.
