Deutsche Telekom invests €100 million in Mistral as a first step toward a sovereign AI partnership

German industry may get a European model supplier delivered through Telekom's own cloud, which matters for companies with strict data rules.

Deutsche Telekom's T.Capital Tech Fund has put €100 million into Mistral's Series D round. Telekom calls the deal a first step toward a strategic partnership, aimed at German businesses and public bodies, especially in industrial and regulated sectors.

Under the plan, Telekom would bring its sovereign infrastructure, customer relationships and integration skills, and Mistral would bring its models, enterprise software and deployment teams. The investment builds on Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud in Munich and on Mistral's new hub in the same city. T-Systems chief Ferri Abolhassan said businesses must be able to trust the technology and keep control of their data.

For automotive suppliers and engineering firms, this points to one more option for AI that keeps data within the EU and runs in German data centres. That could matter for design data, simulation models and supplier documents that cannot go to US clouds.
