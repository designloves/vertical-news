WhatsApp help pages hid AI-only instructions that steered users away from regulators

Content written for AI readers is now a design surface of its own, and it can quietly become a dark pattern.

A researcher saved 545 WhatsApp help pages and found 102 hidden instruction blocks across 80 of them. The blocks were sent with each page but never shown on screen, and were addressed to WhatsApp's support chatbot. Some told the bot not to suggest contacting a data protection regulator when users could not delete their accounts. Others said not to cite privacy laws such as GDPR once a user mentioned a lawyer or a court. Other notes were protective, such as warnings that deleting a group chat can destroy evidence of a scam.

WhatsApp told WIRED the notes were for its support bot, appeared because of a bug, and have been removed. Checks of 300 Facebook and Instagram help pages found nothing similar. The researcher did not test whether the bot actually followed the notes.

For anyone designing AI support flows, the lesson is that instructions to the bot are product copy too. They need the same review, transparency and regulatory scrutiny as visible UI text. In the EU, steering users away from their rights is exactly what consumer and data regulators look for.
