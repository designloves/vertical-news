Pigment and Otter raise big rounds, and Otter's comes with no equity

European AI-native planning is taking on Microsoft head-on, while revenue-based financing offers a new way to fund AI go-to-market.

Paris-based Pigment, which makes business planning software, raised $250 million at a $2.3 billion valuation. The round was led by the EU-backed Scaleup Europe Fund, with Nvidia also investing. Pigment pitches itself as a European alternative to planning work done in Excel and PowerPoint. It is one of the first visible bets from the new public–private scale-up fund.

Otter.ai raised $320 million from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund. General Catalyst takes no equity and instead gets a share of the revenue that the extra sales and marketing spend brings in. Otter reports more than 40 million users and use at over 86% of Fortune 500 companies. It is adding pre-meeting briefs, role-specific tools and an MCP server so other chatbots can read meeting transcripts.

Taken together, these rounds suggest that AI SaaS companies with proven usage can now fund growth without heavy dilution. Europe is also starting to back its own AI application companies, not just infrastructure.
