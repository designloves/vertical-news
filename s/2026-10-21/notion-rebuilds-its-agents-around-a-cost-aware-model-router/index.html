Notion rebuilds its agents around a cost-aware model router and monthly credits

This is a clear template for packaging AI features: auto-routing to cut cost, credits to price usage, and a separate verifier for risky actions.

Notion's sixth-generation agent harness runs each session on a dedicated, queue-backed service, so tasks survive restarts. Agent runtime grows from about five minutes to more than 24 hours. Agents can now write scripts for computer and browser tasks instead of spending model tokens. They run up to 50% faster, and a new "Review for me" mode uses a separate model to check whether an action is safe before the agent takes it.

The bigger change is the business model. An upgraded Auto router sends simple requests to cheap models and hard ones to frontier models, and switches models when one stalls. Notion says this matched top-model quality across 20+ use cases at about 90% lower average cost. Business and Enterprise seats now include 1,500 Standard credits (Notion picks the model) and 500 Select credits (you pick) per month. Existing customers move to the new system on 2 November. ChatGPT accounts can also power a personal Notion agent, and Enterprise customers get bring-your-own-key in alpha.

For product teams, this is now the reference design: hide model choice behind a router, sell tiered credits, and make the safety check visible in the UX.
