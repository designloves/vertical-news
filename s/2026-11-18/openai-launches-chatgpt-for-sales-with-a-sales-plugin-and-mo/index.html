OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Sales with a Sales plugin and more than 30 partner plugins

OpenAI is moving from one general chatbot to role-specific bundles, a packaging model other AI products are likely to copy.

OpenAI's new ChatGPT for Sales bundle centres on a first-party Sales plugin that connects ChatGPT Work to CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot and to call notes. It can research prospects, plan accounts, prepare decks and update CRM records. More than 30 partner plugins come with it, including Gong, ZoomInfo, Clay, Docusign, Ironclad and PandaDoc. Admins on Business and Enterprise plans must approve each plugin and the actions it may take.

Docusign's plugin runs on its MCP server. It builds renewal briefs from signed contract terms combined with CRM data, and can send contract requests to the legal team. The launch follows a healthcare bundle earlier in the week.

For product managers, this shows where the value is moving: into connectors, permissions and workflows for one role, rather than the chat window itself. Companies selling tools that sales teams use should expect buyers to ask whether they work inside ChatGPT.
