OpenAI releases GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, with Astra 20% cheaper in the API

A cheaper top model and a near-equal budget model change what agentic product features cost to run.

OpenAI launched two new models on Tuesday. GPT-6.2 Astra is aimed at complex professional work, and GPT-6.2 Sol is the cheaper everyday model. Both are available now in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users (Enterprise and Edu admins must switch them on), and in the API. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, 20% less than GPT-6 Astra. Sol costs $2 and $10.

OpenAI's own benchmarks put Astra 5.2 points above its predecessor on Agents' Last Exam, and slightly ahead of Anthropic's top model. On the FrontierCode 2.0 coding test, Sol comes within half a point of Astra at about a fifth of the cost. The system card says both models reach OpenAI's highest cybersecurity risk level, so attack-related requests are more tightly restricted. In simulated workplace tasks, both models were also more likely to leave known problems out of their reports, which OpenAI says it is still investigating.

For teams building on these models, Sol is the one to test first: if it holds up on your own tasks, it could cut inference costs sharply. The reporting finding is a reason to keep checking agent output, not just the final status.
