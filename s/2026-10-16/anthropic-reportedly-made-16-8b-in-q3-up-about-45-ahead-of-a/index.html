Anthropic reportedly made $16.8B in Q3, up about 45%, ahead of a November IPO

enterprise AI spending is growing fast, which makes the business case for AI features easier to argue.

The Financial Times, followed by Reuters and Bloomberg, reported that Anthropic told prospective investors its Q3 revenue was $16.8B. That is about 45% above Q2's roughly $11.6B and far above Q1's $4.7B. The figure is real quarterly revenue, not an annualised run rate. Anthropic reportedly said it was profitable on an adjusted basis and cash-flow positive in the quarter, but gave no profit figure. The company has not published the numbers.

According to Bloomberg, IPO marketing could start as early as the week of 9 November, with trading before US Thanksgiving. Some investors talk about a valuation of $1.8–2 trillion.

For Emil: a model vendor that is growing this fast and making money is a safer platform to build on. Its pricing moves, like the recent cuts to small-model prices, also shape what AI features cost to run.
