Microsoft shows MAI-Base-2, a 2.8T-parameter model it trained in-house

Azure and Copilot customers are likely to get Microsoft's own models alongside OpenAI's.

Microsoft AI described MAI-Base-2, a mixture-of-experts foundation model with 2.8 trillion parameters, 80B of them active. It was trained on 50 trillion tokens across 16,000 GB200 GPUs over 38 days in August and September. It handles vision natively without a separate encoder, and its knowledge cutoff is March 2026. Microsoft says its training method is 4.2–12.3 times more compute-efficient than DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base, and that the training data is original rather than distilled from other models.

The model is still in post-training. Microsoft plans to license the weights to trusted partners through Foundry and will not release them publicly. Work on MAI-Base-3 has already started.

For Emil: many large OEMs standardise on Microsoft tooling. A strong first-party Microsoft model means more choice, and probably different prices, inside the Copilot and Azure tools engineering teams already use.
