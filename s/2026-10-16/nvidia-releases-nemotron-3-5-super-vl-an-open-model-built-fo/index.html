Nvidia releases Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open model built for documents

you can self-host extraction from PDFs, tables and charts, such as test reports and spec sheets.

Nvidia published Nemotron 3.5 Super VL. It is a 120B multimodal model that reads PDFs, charts, tables, images and web pages, and it can call tools. It ships under the OpenMDW license, which allows commercial use and redistribution. Nvidia also released the training recipes and datasets. A smaller Bolt 74B-A9B model uses native 2-bit compression to fit in 24 GB of memory, and a 4 GB Bolt E4B is due on 23 October.

Nvidia says that after targeted post-training, the model beats Claude Opus 5 and GPT-6.1 Sol on five specialised tasks, including document intelligence. It has not disclosed how those tasks are defined, so treat the results as vendor claims. Dassault Systèmes and Synopsys are listed as integration partners.

For Emil: in validation work most of the knowledge sits in PDFs. An open model that handles tables and plots well could power internal search or extraction over test reports, without data leaving the company.
