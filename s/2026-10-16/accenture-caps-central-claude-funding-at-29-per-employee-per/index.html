Accenture caps central Claude funding at $29 per employee per month

managing token costs is now a product and pricing problem, not just a finance one.

An internal Accenture memo reported by 404 Media says that from 1 October the company centrally funds only $29 of Claude usage per person per month. Some employees had been spending up to about $1,000 a month. Anything above the cap has to be charged to a specific project code. Premium-plan users still get a weekly token allowance of $250. At least one employee says their access was removed. Accenture says it gives everyone "the right tool for the right work".

This fits a wider pattern. Companies that rolled out agents widely are finding that usage-based bills grow faster than seat licences did.

For Emil: if you design or price AI features, plan for cost ceilings, show users their usage, and decide who pays when someone goes over. The UX of quotas and budgets is becoming a core part of AI products.
