ChatGPT ads triple to about 60,000 advertisers as OpenAI cuts prices

AI chat is turning into a paid acquisition channel, and early clicks are much cheaper than on Google.

The Information reports that OpenAI's ad business has about 60,000 advertisers, up from about 20,000 in late August. The jump follows cuts to published ad prices aimed at small and mid-sized businesses. The ad team has grown to around 140 people, nearly half hired between July and September, many from Meta's ad organisation. One agency said some business-software clicks cost around $5 in ChatGPT, against $30–50 on Google, though results vary by campaign.

The full report is behind a paywall, so these figures come from secondary summaries and OpenAI has not confirmed them.

For Emil: if your products depend on search for discovery, start tracking how they show up in ChatGPT answers and ads. Ads in chat also raise UX questions about how sponsored content is labelled and how far users trust it, and other assistants are likely to face the same questions.
