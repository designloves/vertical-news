FLUX 3 Image Klein: a small open image model with 4K output and layout control

fast, local image generation with layout control, useful for mockups and design assets.

Black Forest Labs, based in Freiburg, released FLUX 3 Image Klein. It is a 5.6B open-weight model for text-to-image, editing and generation from up to 10 reference images. It can output 4K natively. You can use bounding boxes to say where elements go or to limit an edit to one area, and edits leave the rest of the image untouched. The distilled version makes a 1-megapixel image in about 4 seconds on an RTX 5090, and an INT8 build runs on 8 GB consumer GPUs. LoRA fine-tuning works from day one.

The weights are free for commercial use by individuals and companies with less than $5M annual revenue. Larger organisations need a licence or the API. BFL's own tests put it ahead of larger open models, but those are internal numbers.

For Emil: bounding-box placement is the kind of control designers have been asking for. It also makes on-brand imagery possible with a fine-tuned LoRA, without sending assets to a third party.
