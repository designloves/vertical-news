NSK and NTN sign the definitive merger deal: NSK-NTN Ltd. to start on 1 October 2027

Two of the six biggest bearing makers become one, changing the supplier map for OEMs, Tier 1s and industrial buyers.

NSK and NTN have signed a binding business integration agreement, six months after their May memorandum of understanding. The two Japanese bearing makers will form a joint holding company, NSK-NTN Ltd., through a share transfer. Each NSK share becomes one share in the new company, and each NTN share becomes 0.353 shares. Shareholders vote on 25 June 2027, both companies delist on 29 September 2027, and the holding company is due to list on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market on 1 October 2027.

NSK's Akitoshi Ichii will be CEO and NTN's Eiichi Ukai co-CEO, with NSK's Keita Suzuki as CFO and an NTN-nominated non-executive chair. The head office will be in Tokyo, with a second head office in Osaka. The companies target 50–60 billion yen in operating profit synergies by the year to March 2031, mostly from purchasing and logistics (about 40%) and shared production sites (about 30%). Combined net sales were roughly 1.7 trillion yen last financial year.

For European car and truck makers, the deal puts hub bearings, drive shafts, transmission and EV motor bearings under one owner. Buyers who dual-source from NSK and NTN should expect product line and plant consolidation, and possibly new terms. Regulatory approvals are still needed.
