OpenAI tests a redesigned ChatGPT app built around personal agents, but not in the EU or UK

The chat box is turning into app-style navigation around an always-on agent, a pattern other assistants are likely to follow.

OpenAI has started testing a new mobile layout for ChatGPT, shown in a demo by OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux. A bottom tab bar has three parts: Home, with a greeting and an 'Ask anything' field; a dedicated tab for the user's 'dot', an always-on personal agent marked as an early preview; and a grid of tools including Projects, Images, Scheduled, Codex, Plugins, Finance, Health and Sites. Users can press and hold a tool to pin it to the tab bar.

The design is switched on in Settings. The first wave covers adult Pro users plus a small group of Plus users, and dots are also coming to US Business Standard customers. The European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and South Korea are excluded for now, so most of our readers will not see it yet.

For designers, the shift is from one conversation to a home screen with a persistent agent and pinnable tools. It raises familiar questions about discoverability and how much a personal agent should surface on its own. It is a test, not a general release.
