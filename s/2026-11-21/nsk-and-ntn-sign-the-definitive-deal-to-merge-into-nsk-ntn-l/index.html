NSK and NTN sign the definitive deal to merge into NSK-NTN Ltd. from 1 October 2027

Two of the world's largest bearing makers become one owner of hub, transmission and EV motor bearings, which changes sourcing for OEMs and Tier 1s.

NSK and NTN signed a binding business integration agreement on Friday, approved by both boards the same day, six months after their May memorandum of understanding. A joint share transfer will create a holding company, NSK-NTN Ltd., that owns both. Each NSK share becomes one share in the new company and each NTN share becomes 0.353, with about 709 million new shares issued. Shareholders vote on 25 June 2027, both companies delist on 29 September and the holding company lists in Tokyo on 1 October 2027. Foreign competition approvals are still needed.

NSK's Akitoshi Ichii will be CEO and NTN's Eiichi Ukai co-CEO, with an NTN-nominated non-executive chair of a ten-member board and head offices in both Tokyo and Osaka. The target is 50–60 billion yen of operating profit synergies by the year to March 2031: about 40% from purchasing and logistics, 30% from production sites, 20% from admin and R&D and 10% from sales. Combined sales last financial year were about 1.74 trillion yen.

European car and truck makers that dual-source between the two should expect product-line and plant consolidation and new commercial terms. Together with SKF's Vertevo spin-off on 1 December, the automotive bearing supplier map is being redrawn within a year.
