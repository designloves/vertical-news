Anthropic shares fall 8.5% on day two as the greenshoe lifts IPO proceeds to about $93.5 billion

The second day shows how quickly sentiment around AI listings can swing, with knock-on effects for AI pricing and spending.

After closing its first day at 97.17 dollars, Anthropic's stock fell more than 10% before the market opened on Friday, traded around 86 dollars at midday and closed at 88.91 dollars, down 8.5% and only slightly above the 83-dollar IPO price. Coverage pointed to a broad tech sell-off, turmoil at Nvidia and uncertainty around the midterms.

Underwriters exercised their full option to sell 147 million extra shares, about 12.2 billion dollars at the IPO price. That takes IPO proceeds to about 93.5 billion dollars, or roughly 119.5 billion including the earlier 26 billion private placement. The filing showed a third-quarter operating loss of 567 million dollars, partly due to about five billion dollars of compute costs. Bloomberg reports that the stock could join the Nasdaq 100 as soon as December.

For teams that build on Claude, day-to-day share moves change little. The more important thing is that Anthropic now reports publicly, so pricing, capacity and spending will be watched every quarter.
