Canada to take an equity stake of up to $1.1 billion in a Toronto AI company

Governments allied with the US are buying into domestic AI capacity, a trend that also shapes European sovereignty debates.

Semafor reports that Canada will take an equity stake of up to $1.1 billion in a Toronto-based AI startup. Other coverage of the same news names the company as Cohere and puts the commitment at up to C$1.6 billion; Semafor's text itself does not name the company, so treat the identity as reported rather than confirmed.

Semafor frames the move as part of a wider push by US allies to reduce reliance on American technology. It notes the UK is pursuing similar aims, including an $80 million sovereign AI fund and $160 million for a government lab that tests frontier models.

For readers in Europe, the move is a data point in the debate over public funding for homegrown AI, alongside Mistral's open-weight strategy. Details such as governance rights and timing were not available.
