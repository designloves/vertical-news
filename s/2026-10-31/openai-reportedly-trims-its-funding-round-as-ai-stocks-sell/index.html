OpenAI reportedly trims its funding round as AI stocks sell off

Funding for AI products is tightening, so vendors your teams depend on may change pricing and roadmaps.

CoinDesk, citing the Financial Times, reports that OpenAI has lowered its fundraising target from $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation to about $20 billion at $1.25 trillion. OpenAI had already pushed its IPO plans to 2027. This is a media report, not an OpenAI announcement.

The backdrop is a sharp market move. The Nasdaq 100 is down about 10 percent in roughly three weeks from its 6 October record, including a 4.5 percent fall on Friday. Rival Anthropic is reported to plan a November listing aiming to raise up to $100 billion at a $1.5 trillion valuation, which is also unconfirmed.

For companies building on these models, the risk is indirect but real: less cheap capital can mean fewer subsidised prices, tighter usage limits and a stronger push toward profitable enterprise tiers. It is a good moment to check how dependent a product is on one model vendor.
