Humanoid moves its robots onto a single Nvidia Jetson Thor computer

Simulation-trained robots with consolidated onboard compute show how virtual development and edge AI are converging in industrial robotics.

UK robotics company Humanoid is expanding its work with Nvidia to build an edge runtime so its next-generation robots can run AI on board. It will use Jetson Thor with Isaac OS and the Holoscan Sensor Bridge and SDK.

The move lets Humanoid merge two onboard computers into one while keeping its computing capacity, with room to grow. Multi-gigabit Ethernet links carry up to 100 Gb of bandwidth for sensor data and control. The company has used Nvidia's Isaac Lab and Isaac Sim since its early days to train and simulate models before deploying them on physical robots.

For industrial and automotive engineers, this is the same workflow as virtual validation: train and test in simulation, then deploy to a leaner hardware stack. Fewer boxes also means fewer failure points to validate.
