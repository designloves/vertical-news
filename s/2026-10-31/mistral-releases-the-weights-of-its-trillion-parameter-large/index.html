Mistral releases the weights of its trillion-parameter Large 4

A European lab now offers a frontier-scale model that companies can run on their own servers, which matters for data-sensitive engineering and product teams.

French lab Mistral has published the weights of Mistral Large 4, a multimodal model with about a trillion parameters. Organisations can download it from Hugging Face, call it through Mistral's API, or deploy it on their own infrastructure. Mistral aims it at software development and defensive cybersecurity, and pitches it as a sovereign option for enterprises and governments.

The performance claims come from Mistral, not independent tests. The company says Large 4 matches GLM-5.3, a Chinese open-weight model, while using 47 percent fewer tokens per task, and that it tops all other open models on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. The licence terms were not clear in the coverage we could read, so check them before planning commercial use.

For European product and engineering teams, the practical point is control. A self-hosted model of this class lets companies keep source code, test data and design files inside their own perimeter, which is often a precondition in automotive and supplier environments.
