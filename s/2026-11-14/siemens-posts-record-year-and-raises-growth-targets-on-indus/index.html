Siemens posts record year and raises growth targets on industrial AI

The owner of Simcenter, Altair and Teamcenter is betting its growth on AI-driven engineering software, which will shape the CAE and PLM tools OEMs rely on.

Siemens reported record results for fiscal 2026. Comparable revenue rose 8% to 83.4 billion euros, orders rose 25% to 108.2 billion euros and the order backlog reached a record 143 billion euros. Digital business revenue grew 16% to 10.9 billion euros, and Digital Industries software revenue reached 7.2 billion euros, up 13% on a comparable basis. CEO Roland Busch described industrial AI as an exceptional growth opportunity.

In the fourth quarter, the Digital Industries margin rose to 20.9%. Siemens raised its mid-term revenue growth target to 7–11% from 6–9% and still aims to double digital revenue by fiscal 2030. Its investor slides cite an industrial customer reporting engineering twice as fast with Siemens' engineering agent and say revenue synergies from the Altair and Dotmatics acquisitions are starting, without giving figures.

For simulation and test engineers, the signal is that Siemens will keep pushing AI agents and Altair's solvers deeper into Simcenter and Teamcenter. Expect AI features to become a bigger part of licence negotiations.
