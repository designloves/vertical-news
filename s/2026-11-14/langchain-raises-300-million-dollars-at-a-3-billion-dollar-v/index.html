LangChain raises 300 million dollars at a 3 billion dollar valuation to sell agent governance

Money is flowing to the layer that controls, budgets and audits AI agents, which teams need before agents go into production.

LangChain has raised a 300 million dollar Series C led by CapitalG, with GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP and Sapphire Ventures taking part. The 3 billion dollar valuation is more than double the 1.25 billion dollars of its previous round about a year ago.

The company started in 2022 as an open-source framework for connecting language models to search, APIs and databases. Its business now centres on LangSmith, a platform for building, running and governing agents that is not tied to any model or cloud. LangChain claims more than 7,000 LangSmith customer organisations, including Harvey, Rippling, NVIDIA, Toyota and Salesforce. New agent governance features centralise control over which models, tools and data agents can use, with gateways that set model budgets and fallbacks, protect sensitive data and manage tool access. No revenue figures were disclosed.

For product managers, the round confirms that observability, cost control and permissions are becoming separate products. Teams planning agent features should budget for this layer instead of treating it as an afterthought.
