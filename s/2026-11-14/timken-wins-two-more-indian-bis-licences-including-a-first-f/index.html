Timken wins two more Indian BIS licences, including a first for double-row cylindrical roller bearings

Indian certification is becoming a gate for bearing suppliers into one of the fastest-growing vehicle and industrial markets.

Timken has received two new licences from the Bureau of Indian Standards for its bearing plants in Bharuch, Gujarat. One, under IS 6454, covers double-row cylindrical roller bearings at its new Bharuch facility; Timken says it is the first licence for this bearing type granted to any bearing company in India. The other, under IS 6457, covers cylindrical roller bearings at its existing plant.

The approvals bring Timken's total to eight BIS licences in 2026, which the company says is the most awarded to any bearing maker in India this year. India has been tightening quality-control rules that require many bearings sold in the country to carry BIS certification.

For OEMs and suppliers sourcing in India, locally certified production of more bearing types makes it easier to qualify Indian-made parts for vehicles, rail and industrial machinery, and raises the bar for importers that lack certification.
