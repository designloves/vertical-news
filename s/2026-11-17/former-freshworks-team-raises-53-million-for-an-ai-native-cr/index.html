Former Freshworks team raises $53 million for an AI-native CRM aimed at Salesforce and HubSpot

Start-ups are rebuilding core business software around agents rather than adding AI to old data models, and some are already winning customers from incumbents.

Dreamteam, founded in 2025 in San Francisco by three former Freshworks employees with engineering in Chennai, has raised $53 million across a seed round led by Together Fund and a Series A led by Accel, with Premji Invest also taking part. The product combines a shared company record built from email, meetings and customer interactions; four built-in agents that research prospects, draft follow-ups, flag deal risks and configure workflows, with humans approving anything sent to customers; and a tool that builds custom apps and dashboards from plain-language requests.

The company says it has more than 100 paying customers in 13 countries, all signed within three months of starting to sell. It has replaced Salesforce at DMS Health and HubSpot at other customers, and it targets eight-figure annual recurring revenue by the end of 2027. Pricing was not disclosed. The founders estimate a two-to-five-year window before incumbents fully adapt.

For product strategists, the human-approval step for customer-facing output is a design choice worth noting: it is how agent-first products are winning trust in sales teams.
