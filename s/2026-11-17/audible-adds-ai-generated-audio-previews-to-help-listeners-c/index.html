Audible adds AI-generated audio previews to help listeners choose audiobooks

Summarising reviews with AI raises a design question about balance: whose opinions get surfaced, and whether negative feedback disappears.

Audible has launched an audio-first discovery format. Each preview is a short spoken introduction that combines an AI-generated overview of the book's themes, a summary of what readers liked, commentary from editors or authors taken from past Audible interviews, and the narrated sample. It starts with selected English titles in the US.

In December, Audible will extend Recaps and its interactive Ask a Question feature to more US titles, limited to people who own the book or have it through a membership. TechCrunch points out a risk: summaries built mostly from positive reviews could hide critical feedback. The launch comes a month after Spotify expanded audiobooks to more than 180 markets.

For product designers, it is a concrete example of AI summaries sitting at the decision point in a purchase flow, where transparency about sources and sentiment matters most.
