Hugging Face hands all its language-model inference routing to OpenRouter

Its numbers show open-weight models now handle most routed tokens at a fraction of closed-model prices, which changes the cost assumptions behind AI features.

Hugging Face Inference Providers now sends all language-model requests through OpenRouter instead of its own router. OpenRouter picks a provider for each call by price, speed and reliability and fails over automatically. Existing code keeps working, including shortcuts for the fastest or cheapest provider. Users pay provider list prices on their Hugging Face bill with no markup, but custom provider API keys are no longer supported for language models. Image, video and audio requests are unchanged.

The announcement includes market figures. OpenRouter says it serves more than 800 trillion tokens a month to over 10 million users across 600-plus models. Open-weight models rose from 26.8% of its tokens in October 2025 to 71.7% in September 2026. Their average price is $0.28 per million input tokens and $1.62 per million output tokens, against $1.27 and $6.36 for closed models.

For product managers pricing AI features, this is strong evidence that routing and model choice are becoming commodity infrastructure, and that margins depend increasingly on picking the cheapest model good enough for each task.
