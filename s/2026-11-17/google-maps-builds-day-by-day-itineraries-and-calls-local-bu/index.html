Google Maps builds day-by-day itineraries and calls local businesses to find appointments

A chat answer becomes a lasting, editable plan with an agent acting behind it, a pattern product teams are increasingly expected to support.

Ask Maps, the Gemini-based chat feature in Google Maps, can now build day-by-day itineraries with numbered pins on the map. Plans can be shared or exported to Google Docs, and they appear during navigation with live traffic and opening hours. A 'know before you go' feature adds route tips on parking, EV charging, weather, delays and tolls. Users can also search their saved lists by live availability, for example a table for five within 30 minutes.

A new Find Appointments feature searches online or phones local businesses in beauty, pet care, car repair and home services to find open slots, starting in the US. Ask Maps is also expanding to 30 more European countries, including Germany, Spain and the UK. Google did not say whether the AI identifies itself on calls or which languages it supports.

For designers, the useful detail is how the chat output becomes a persistent object with its own place in the app. The EV charging tips are a reminder that in-car navigation and phone apps are converging.
