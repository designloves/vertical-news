Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast everyday model at $1.50 per million input tokens

A cheap model that comes close to Google's top tier changes the cost of building agent features into products, and it now powers Google Search for everyone.

Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen, which it describes as a fast model for everyday agent work, sitting below the larger Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon models. It costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, with the same price across its full 1 million token context window. It can also write up to 1 million output tokens. Google reports a score of 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default settings, slightly above Argon, and 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1. Google also says it makes far fewer made-up claims than Gemini 3.8 Flash.

Hydrogen is available now in the Gemini API, AI Studio, Android Studio, the Gemini app and Google Search, and it is coming to Sheets. Google's Antigravity coding tool gains an Orchestrator that switches between Hydrogen and Carbon within one session. Google says this gets close to Carbon's success rate on Terminal-Bench 4.0 while using 24% less quota.

For product teams, model routing (a strong model plans, cheaper ones do the work) is becoming a built-in feature rather than something each team builds itself. All benchmark figures are Google's own.
