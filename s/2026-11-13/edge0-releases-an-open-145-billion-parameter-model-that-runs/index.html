Edge0 releases an open 145-billion-parameter model that runs on a laptop

Strong models that run locally make offline, private AI features realistic for desktop apps and engineering tools.

Edge0, a company that builds AI models for running on devices, released Edge0-145B and Edge0-120B for laptops, along with smaller models for phones, speech and embeddings. The 145B model uses a mixture-of-experts design that activates only about 10 billion parameters at each step. Edge0 says it needs about 29GB of memory at peak and generates more than 80 tokens per second. It targets 48GB laptops on macOS with Apple silicon, Windows, Linux and high-end Nvidia RTX cards.

On Edge0's own tests the model averages 72.8 across six benchmarks, slightly ahead of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and DeepSeek-V4-Flash. It scores 90.4 on GPQA-Diamond. The weights are on Hugging Face and GitHub, but the announcement does not name a licence.

For product and front-end teams, local models remove per-token costs and data-transfer concerns. This matters for regulated industries and for engineering data that cannot leave the company. Check the licence and independent benchmarks before relying on it.
