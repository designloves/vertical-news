Murata plans ¥1 trillion over five years for capacitors aimed at AI servers

Passive components are a quiet bottleneck for AI hardware, and the same parts go into vehicle electronics.

Nikkei Asia reports that Murata Manufacturing will invest about ¥1 trillion (roughly $6.3 billion) over five years to expand output of multilayer ceramic capacitors and other advanced electronic components. Capacity will be added in Japan, the Philippines, China and Thailand.

Murata is responding to surging data-centre demand for these components, though Nikkei notes some observers question whether the AI boom will last. The accessible part of the article gave no plant-level capacity figures or schedule.

Capacitors of this type are also used in vehicle electronics, so heavy AI-server demand can tighten supply for automotive buyers. Engineering and purchasing teams may want to check lead times and second sources.
