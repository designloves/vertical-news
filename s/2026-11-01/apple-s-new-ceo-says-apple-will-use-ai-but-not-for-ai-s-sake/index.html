Apple's new CEO says Apple will use AI but not 'for AI's sake'

A clear statement of product philosophy from the company that ships AI to more consumers than almost anyone.

In an interview with Wired, reported by TechCrunch, Apple CEO John Ternus, who took over in early September, said Apple will use AI in new products but should not add AI features just for their own sake. He said success is when users do not need to think about whether AI or ordinary software did the work.

Asked whether AI agents could make apps unnecessary, he said that might simplify some things, but that making products is human and personal work Apple enjoys. Apple has been seen as behind in AI, partly because a more capable Siri was delayed until this summer.

For product and UX teams, the stance supports an invisible-AI approach: judge a feature by whether it makes the task easier, not by whether it is labelled as AI.
