Judge lets NAACP's Clean Air Act case against xAI's Memphis data centre go ahead

Power supply for AI sites is becoming a legal question, which matters for anyone planning compute capacity.

A federal judge, Debra M. Brown, denied xAI's motion to dismiss a Clean Air Act lawsuit brought by the NAACP. The suit says xAI ran at least 35 combustion turbines and other sources at its Colossus data centre near Memphis without the required air permits.

xAI argued that the turbines were mobile and so exempt from permits, that the plaintiffs lacked standing, and that citizen suits are unconstitutional. The court rejected all three. TechCrunch reports that a similar motion from the Department of Justice was denied earlier in October. TechCrunch had asked SpaceX, which owns xAI, for comment and reported no reply.

The ruling does not decide the merits, but it keeps the case alive. Data-centre operators that use on-site gas generation to avoid grid delays may face more permit challenges.
