Apple will put Alibaba's Qwen inside Apple Intelligence in China

A Western device maker is putting a Chinese model at the core of its system AI, which shows how regional AI stacks are splitting.

Alibaba said on Saturday that its Qwen model will be built into Apple Intelligence for users in China. Qwen will power Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence. Developers can test it in the iOS developer beta from 4 November, and it reaches the public with iOS 27.3 in December.

Users can choose whether to switch Qwen on and what to share. Alibaba says it will not use requests or responses to train its models. The feature is limited to Apple Intelligence-capable iPhones bought in China, or devices set to China for both location and Apple Account region. Chinese regulators had already completed filings for seven on-device generative AI services in July, Apple Intelligence among them.

For product teams, this is a clear case of one app experience running on different model backends by market. Anyone designing AI features for global users should plan for models that differ in behaviour, tone and rules by region, and test them separately.
