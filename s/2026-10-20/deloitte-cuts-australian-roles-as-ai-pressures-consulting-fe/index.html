Deloitte cuts Australian roles as AI pressures consulting fees; auditors launch an agent certification

Professional services are repricing around AI, and 'certified agents' may become a procurement checkbox for B2B products.

Deloitte Australia cut about 170 roles while hiring 500+ into its outsourcing arm, according to the Australian Financial Review. Internally, the firm estimates AI could automate a third of routine consulting work and push partners to cut fees by up to 40%.

In the same news cycle, the AICPA and the Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company announced a joint assurance offering that combines a SOC 2 report with AIUC-1, a standard for AI agent safety, security and reliability, in a single CPA-led engagement. Several firms are already running it, with a full launch expected in early 2027.

For anyone selling AI features to enterprises, expect agent audits to join SOC 2 and ISO 27001 in security questionnaires, and plan for the logging, evaluation and controls that auditors will ask to see.
