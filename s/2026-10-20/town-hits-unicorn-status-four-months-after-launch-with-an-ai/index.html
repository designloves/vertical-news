Town hits unicorn status four months after launch with an AI assistant per employee

Shows investor appetite for the 'agent per seat' model, where assistants share context across a company.

Town, founded by former Plaid CTO Jean-Denis Greze, raised a $108M Series B co-led by Forerunner and Menlo Ventures, with a16z and First Round joining, at a valuation above $1B. It has raised over $160M in total, including a $55M Series A in June, and reached millions in ARR within weeks of its public launch.

Every employee gets an assistant (a 'Townie') connected to email, calendar and core work systems. Townies can talk to each other, share approved information and now also deal with customers outside the company. A new shared workflow pushes business updates to all assistants at once.

The product question it raises is coordination: once each person has an agent, the hard design work becomes permissions, shared memory and which agent is allowed to say what to whom. The Muse incident above shows what happens when that is underdesigned.
