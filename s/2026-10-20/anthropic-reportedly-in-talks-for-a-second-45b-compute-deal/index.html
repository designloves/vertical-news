Anthropic reportedly in talks for a second ~$45B compute deal with Nscale

Compute contracts this size shape which models get built, and what API capacity and pricing look like in 2027–28.

Bloomberg reported, and Reuters confirmed with a source, that Anthropic is negotiating to rent a second building at Nscale's Monarch campus in West Virginia: about 460 MW running Nvidia Vera Rubin chips, worth roughly $45B. Anthropic already signed a similar ~$45B deal for the first building in August and holds first refusal on a planned third. Talks are ongoing and terms could change; Anthropic did not comment.

The news lands in a shaky week for AI infrastructure. Microsoft has walked away from anchoring a flagship Nscale data centre after grid connection delays pushed power into the 2030s, and investors have reportedly pushed both Nscale and Anthropic to lower IPO valuation targets. Nscale's contracted backlog has nonetheless grown to around $100B in about two and a half years.

The takeaway for product teams: frontier labs are locking in capacity years ahead, which supports continued model improvements and steadier API supply, but also ties lab finances to a small number of very large, leveraged infrastructure bets.
