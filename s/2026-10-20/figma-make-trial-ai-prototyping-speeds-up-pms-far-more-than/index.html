Figma Make trial: AI prototyping speeds up PMs far more than designers

Evidence that prompt-to-design tools narrow the skill gap between PMs and designers; it changes who does early exploration.

Jakob Nielsen's weekly roundup highlighted a randomized controlled trial run by Figma researchers and moderated by MeasuringU. 50 product designers and 50 product managers completed three standard design tasks with or without Figma Make. Overall completion was about 20% faster with AI; PMs saved around 35% of time versus 17% for designers, and PMs' usability rating of Figma rose about 15 points with AI. A caveat: participants were shown the target design, so design judgement was not tested.

A separate survey of 443 designers found AI use drops steadily through the Double Diamond: 72% use it in Discover, 41% in Deliver, and only about a third of AI users apply it in every phase. Doubt about usefulness, not ease of use, separated holdouts from adopters.

Read together: AI makes producing screens cheap, especially for non-designers, so a designer's value shifts toward framing, critique and systems, the parts the trial did not measure.
