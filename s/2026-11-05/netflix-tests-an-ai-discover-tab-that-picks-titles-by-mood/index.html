Netflix tests an AI 'Discover' tab that picks titles by mood

A mainstream example of an AI-native browsing pattern: themed prompts and feedback controls instead of a static grid.

Netflix is rolling out an AI Discover tab to US English-language users on supported TVs, with wider access planned in the coming weeks. The first feature suggests titles by mood, using curated themes such as 'something light', 'scare me' or 'quick watches', and shows clips from shows and films.

Users can narrow a theme or choose 'something different' to start over, and each title has a 'Tune Your Recommendations' button to save favourites or stop similar suggestions. Netflix says a new AI layer responds to what users tell it in real time. It calls the tab its 'test kitchen', where it will gather feedback before moving features into the main app.

The design lesson is the separation of an experimental space from the core experience, plus lightweight, always-available correction controls.
