Fal doubles its valuation to $16 billion on a $1.3 billion round

Inference providers are becoming a major layer of the AI stack for teams building image, video and audio features.

Fal, which hosts and runs AI image, video and audio models, raised $1.3 billion in a Series F led by Bessemer, with Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price joining. The $16 billion valuation is twice its April level of $8 billion. Series E and F together total $1.65 billion.

Bloomberg reports annualised revenue passed $800 million earlier this year. Rivals Baseten, Fireworks AI and Modal are also growing quickly, although TechCrunch notes their gross margins are thin because compute is expensive.

For product teams, it shows where generative media features are being built: on shared inference platforms rather than in-house. Costs and rate limits at these providers will influence what is practical to ship.
