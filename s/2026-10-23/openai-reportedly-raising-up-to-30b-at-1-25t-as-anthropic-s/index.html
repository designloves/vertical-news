OpenAI reportedly raising up to $30B at $1.25T, as Anthropic's public S-1 may land today

The two labs whose models you build on are about to publish audited numbers, which will shape pricing and roadmap stability.

CNBC reports that OpenAI is negotiating a raise of up to $30B at a $1.25T pre-money valuation, led by Coatue with UAE investors. The round may be paired with additional UAE compute capacity. Terms are still under negotiation, and OpenAI declined to comment. At that level it would be the most valuable private company ever. OpenAI filed confidential IPO papers in June.

Anthropic, which also filed confidentially in June, could make its S-1 public as early as Friday 23 October, according to Yahoo Finance. That would open the way to a November listing. Reported figures include $16.3B in first-half revenue and $16.8B in Q3. Most of the reported $80B+ first-half net loss comes from a $72B non-cash charge tied to convertible bonds. The company says it is profitable on an adjusted basis. Valuation talk ranges from about $1.2T to $2T.

None of this is confirmed by the companies yet. Watch the public filing for usage, margin and compute-commitment details.
