DeepSeek raises $15B ahead of a planned Shanghai IPO, with CATL and Geely among backers

A cheap, high-margin open-model provider with automotive investors keeps price pressure on every API you pay for.

The Wall Street Journal reports that DeepSeek closed a $15B second private round, nearly double its target, at roughly a $74B pre-money valuation. New backers include Tencent, battery maker CATL and carmaker Geely, alongside Legend Capital and Stony Creek. The company has hired Citic Securities and is preparing a Shanghai listing for early 2027, which could put it on public markets before OpenAI.

Founder Liang Wenfeng reportedly told investors that annualised revenue reached $1B in September, up from $400–500M in July, driven mainly by API sales. Gross margins on its V4 flagship are reported at 70–80% despite prices below US rivals. DeepSeek still trains its hardest workloads on Nvidia chips, but it is moving more training to Huawei hardware.

Annualised revenue does not equal profit, and the IPO will bring more scrutiny. The CATL and Geely stakes are worth noting, though: Chinese automotive players are tying themselves directly to a frontier model lab.
