Frontier AI found two critical PHP flaws before attackers, prompting emergency patching

PHP runs a large share of the web. AI-speed vulnerability discovery means patch cycles for your products need to get shorter.

PHP's maintainers released fixes for two critical vulnerabilities that could allow remote code execution. According to OpenAI, security firm Calif found one of the flaws using GPT-5.6 Sol as part of the Patch the Planet initiative, and Wiz independently found and reported both. OpenAI, Wiz and Cloudflare helped coordinate a disclosure process that ran for about two months. Reports said some European public-sector sites were briefly taken offline while they patched.

OpenAI calls the gap before disclosure a Defender's Window and says it expects such AI-driven findings to rise sharply. It is also releasing a PHP version checker as a Codex skill, offering Codex for OSS to maintainers, and committing $1B in subsidised access to its Daybreak security tooling for under-resourced organisations that protect essential services.

For product teams, the takeaway is practical: inventory your dependencies and how quickly you can patch them. When models can find bugs this fast, the time between disclosure and exploitation shrinks too.
