Volkswagen Group puts Copilot in the hands of 330,000 employees, with 26,000 agents active

This is adoption data from a European OEM at full scale, a benchmark for internal AI rollouts in automotive.

Microsoft says Volkswagen Group has equipped more than 330,000 employees with Copilot. The phased rollout began with Copilot Chat, which reached more than 300,000 monthly active users, and has moved on to Copilot with Work IQ for more advanced scenarios. More than 26,000 agents were active across the group in the past month. The case was presented at Microsoft's Frontier Tour in Cologne on 22 October.

The two companies will also scale VW's Intelligent Data Foundation, its group-wide data platform, on Microsoft Fabric. Microsoft says AI is embedded in processes from engineering to production, procurement and sales, but it named no specific R&D use cases.

The useful signal is the order of the rollout: broad chat access first, then context-aware assistants, then employee-built agents on a unified data layer. A Swedish OEM or supplier planning similar programmes can use the 26,000-agent figure when discussing governance and agent sprawl.
