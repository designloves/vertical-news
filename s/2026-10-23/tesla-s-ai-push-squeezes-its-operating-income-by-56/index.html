Tesla's AI push squeezes its operating income by 56%

AI and robotics R&D is now visibly eating into an automaker's margins, and investors are watching whether it pays off.

Tesla's Q3 revenue was flat at $28.05B, and adjusted EPS of $0.48 beat expectations. Operating income fell 56% to $718M, however, and operating margin dropped to 2.6%. R&D rose 69% to $2.76B, which Tesla attributes to what it calls Super Intelligence, the Optimus robot and Cybercab. Quarterly capex more than doubled to $4.73B, and free cash flow fell to $1.53B.

On autonomy, unsupervised FSD for private owners slipped to Q2 2027 and will be limited to Robotaxi zones. The Robotaxi fleet has passed nearly 2 million unsupervised miles. FSD subscriptions rose 67% to 1.74M. Tesla also said battery supply, not demand, limited vehicle volumes.

Together with GM's plan and Meta's capex hike this week, the pattern is clear: the companies betting hardest on AI are accepting thinner margins now for platform advantages later.
