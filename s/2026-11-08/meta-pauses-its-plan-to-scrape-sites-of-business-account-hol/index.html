Meta pauses its plan to scrape sites of business-account holders after a European backlash

A live test of how far AI companies can go in using web content, with UK and EU-adjacent regulators involved.

Meta has paused, with no new date, proposed commercial terms that would let it scrape websites of Meta Business account holders, including news publishers. The terms had already slipped from 30 October to 20 November. After criticism Meta removed the word 'train' and limited use to advertising and business products, but the pushback continued.

Nieman Lab reports that courts in Norway and Brazil issued preliminary injunctions after suits by media groups, and the UK Competition and Markets Authority opened a formal investigation into possible abuse of dominance. UK and German politicians and US senators from both parties also objected. Existing terms stay in force.

For anyone publishing content or running a site that uses Meta's business tools, it is worth checking what the current terms allow, since the pause is not a withdrawal.
