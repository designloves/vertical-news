METR estimates Anthropic's AI progress is running at about 1.9x its earlier pace

An outside assessment of how much AI now speeds up AI research bears on how fast tools for designers and engineers may improve.

METR published an independent assessment of AI-driven research speed-up at Anthropic as of October. On benchmark scores for well-defined tasks, it estimates progress is about 1.9 times the pre-October-2025 rate, with a 95% range of 1.5 to 2.8. It guesses that roughly 80% of the acceleration comes from AI assistance, and it is confident that faster growth in training compute is not the cause.

METR is open about limits. Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative, so outsiders cannot check much of it. The benchmarks measure narrow, automatically graded tasks and some are near saturation. Using non-Anthropic models, the acceleration is about 1.4x. Informal staff polls showed a self-reported 12x uplift, which METR calls very loose evidence.

Treat the figure as a careful estimate, not a measurement. If it holds, expect release cycles to keep shortening, which affects how long any design or engineering workflow built on a specific model stays current.
