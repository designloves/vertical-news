AI-assisted proof of a 2008 quantum-computing conjecture is posted, not yet peer reviewed

A sign of AI systems contributing to real research problems, with an important caveat about review.

Physical Superintelligence (PSI) says its research system, running a non-public Anthropic model, found a full proof of the Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture, open since 2008. The paper was posted on GitHub by PSI's David Turturean, who says he reviewed it and takes responsibility for its correctness.

The main theorem was checked in the Lean proof assistant, and some Stanford researchers reportedly read the proof, according to PSI's own account. No peer-reviewed publication has appeared. The result concerns how many input reads a quantum algorithm needs for most random inputs. It does not set a general limit on quantum speed, and the read count can still be large.

Humans chose the problem and operated the system, so it does not show how often such tools can solve open problems. It is worth following as an example of formal verification being used to check AI-generated mathematics.
