OpenAI reportedly raises $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation

The size of the round shows how much capital is still flowing into frontier labs, and what that pressure may mean for product pricing.

Bloomberg reported on Friday, and Reuters repeated it, that OpenAI has closed $11 billion in new funding as the first part of a round valuing the company at $1.25 trillion. The investors were not named in the article we could read.

Other coverage says the full round could grow to around $20 billion, but that figure was not confirmed in the primary report and OpenAI had not issued its own statement in the sources we checked. The news is based on people familiar with the matter.

For product teams the practical point is that the biggest model vendors keep raising money at very high valuations, which supports continued price competition and rapid feature releases. For European buyers it also raises the usual questions about vendor dependence and where data is processed.
