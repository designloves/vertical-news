Stellantis tells suppliers to map their supply chains down to the part

Part-level traceability demands will cascade to tier-2 and tier-3 suppliers, including component makers such as bearing producers.

Stellantis will require suppliers to map their extended supply chains down to individual parts, according to Automotive News as summarised by OICA. It will start with semiconductors and other components judged high-risk. The aim is to spot weak points earlier, after years of shortages and disruptions.

Suppliers have raised concerns about the cost of building and maintaining such maps, the confidentiality of their own sub-suppliers, and legal exposure if the data is shared. The full article is behind a paywall, so details on deadlines and scope are not confirmed here.

If the policy spreads to other OEMs, expect more requests for sub-tier data from all component makers. Teams in sourcing and engineering may need tooling that links part numbers, materials and plants, and may face the same questions on steel, rare materials and electronics.
