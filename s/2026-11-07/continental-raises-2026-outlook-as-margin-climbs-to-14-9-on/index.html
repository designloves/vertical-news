Continental raises 2026 outlook as margin climbs to 14.9% on lower sales

A major European supplier is improving profit through mix and cost control even as volumes stay weak, a template other suppliers are measured against.

Continental reported third-quarter sales from continuing operations of €3,454 million, down from €3,898 million a year earlier. Organic sales fell 1.5% once the prior-year OESL business is excluded. Adjusted EBIT rose to €514 million from €482 million, lifting the margin to 14.9% from 13.9%.

By region, EMEA grew 2.2% organically with an 18.7% margin, while the Americas fell 7.4% organically with an 8.7% margin. The company narrowed its full-year sales range to €13.5–13.9 billion and raised its adjusted EBIT margin guidance to 13.4–14.0%, from 12.0–13.5%. Free cash flow guidance is now €1.0–1.3 billion. The ContiTech sale is expected to close around year-end, subject to approvals.

The result shows that suppliers with a premium mix can protect margin while volumes are flat. Contrast it with Schaeffler's restructuring announced this week: the supply base is splitting between companies adjusting from strength and those cutting capacity.
