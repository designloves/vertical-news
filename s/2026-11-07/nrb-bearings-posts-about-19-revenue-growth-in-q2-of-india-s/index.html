NRB Bearings posts about 19% revenue growth in Q2 of India's fiscal 2027

A mid-sized Indian bearing maker growing fast signals strong industrial and automotive demand in the region, with margins improving.

NRB Bearings reported results for the quarter ended 30 September 2026. Consolidated revenue from operations rose about 19% year on year to ₹756 crore, though the management quote in the report cites 18.8%. Normalised EBITDA grew 23.5%.

The report puts EBITDA at 18.6% of sales for the first half of the year. It gives no profit figure for the quarter. A separate headline says the company announced a joint venture with Italian industrial bearing maker Unitec, but the sources reviewed give no terms, so details are unconfirmed.

For bearing and drivetrain engineers, the figures are a regional demand signal rather than a technology story. Indian production of automotive and industrial components continues to expand, and suppliers there are adding partnerships with European specialists.
