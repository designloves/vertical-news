ChatGPT starts merging chat and work tasks onto one shared usage limit

A pricing signal for AI products: agent-style work is being folded into existing plans under a single meter.

OpenAI has begun rolling out a combined chat and work experience in ChatGPT to a small group of Pro subscribers. Both modes now draw on one usage allowance instead of separate ones. Conversations with the Instant model stay unlimited and do not count against it, so users can keep chatting after the shared limit runs out. OpenAI says shared limits will reach more users in December.

A spokesperson said most people should not hit limits more often, but a small share of the heaviest users may reach them sooner. Some subscribers have complained that they could lose access to advanced reasoning models while paying the same price.

Anthropic made a similar move in September when it merged Claude chat and Cowork. For product managers, the shift shows vendors moving from feature-based tiers to shared compute budgets, which makes usage design and limit messaging part of the core product experience.
