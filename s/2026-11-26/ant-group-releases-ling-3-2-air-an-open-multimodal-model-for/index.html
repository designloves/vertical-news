Ant Group releases Ling-3.2-air, an open multimodal model for office automation, under the MIT licence

Capable open-weight models for document and browser tasks give teams in Europe more options for running agents on their own infrastructure.

Ant Group has published the weights of Ling-3.2-air on Hugging Face under the MIT licence, which allows commercial use and modification. The model handles text, images and video and is aimed at agents that use software tools for professional work, such as producing documents, working in office software and completing browser tasks.

It uses a hybrid linear-attention mixture-of-experts design. The language part has 124 billion parameters in total, with 5.5 billion active for each token, which keeps running costs down. Ant includes deployment instructions for SGLang and vLLM. On its own office-automation test, AutomationBench-AA, Ant says the task completion rate rose to 61% from 15.7% for the earlier Ling-3.0-flash-VL.

The benchmark is Ant's own and has not been checked independently. Teams should also weigh their policies on Chinese-origin models, which some US companies are now reconsidering.
