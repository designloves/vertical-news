ChatGPT moves chat and work tasks onto one shared usage meter

Shared limits across modes are becoming the norm for AI subscriptions, which affects how teams plan seats and heavy agent use.

OpenAI has started rolling out a combined Chat and Work experience in ChatGPT to a small group of Pro subscribers. The two modes will draw on one usage allowance instead of separate ones. Conversations using the Instant model stay unlimited and do not count against it, so users can keep chatting with Instant after the shared limit runs out. OpenAI's support page says shared limits will reach more users in December.

A spokesperson said most people should not hit resets more often, but a small share of the heaviest users may reach limits sooner. Some subscribers have complained on social media and OpenAI's forum that they could lose access to advanced reasoning models while paying the same price. Anthropic made a similar move in September, when it merged Claude chat and Cowork into one interface.

For product managers, this is a pricing signal. Vendors are bundling agent-style work into existing plans and managing cost through shared meters rather than raising list prices.
