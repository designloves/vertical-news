Meta pushes its Muse shopping agent into Instagram DMs while negotiating access to Amazon

Agent commerce is becoming a fight over distribution, ad revenue and who controls the customer relationship.

Meta has brought its Muse AI assistant directly into Instagram direct messages and teamed up with Walmart to offer Black Friday deals through the agent. At the same time, Fortune reports, as covered by The Decoder on 25 November, that Amazon and Meta are negotiating an end to Amazon's September block on Muse.

The sticking points are advertising revenue and control over search results. Amazon's ad business brings in about 68.6 billion dollars a year, and the company worries that outside agents could bypass its ads. Muse has more than 10 million users in the US and Canada, and one industry data source puts its share of agent-placed orders at about half. Any deal would need approval from both chief executives.

For product teams building agent features, the lesson is that access terms, attribution and revenue sharing with large platforms will decide what agents can actually do.
