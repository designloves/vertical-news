J.P. Morgan downgrades Volvo Cars to underweight and backs trucks, including AB Volvo, for 2027

The bank's 2027 outlook splits Sweden's two Volvos, favouring heavy trucks over volume car makers.

In its 2027 outlook for European autos, J.P. Morgan said trucks, luxury cars and tyres should lead the sector. Its preferred names are Ferrari, Porsche, Michelin, Continental, Daimler Truck and AB Volvo. It cut Volvo Cars to underweight from neutral with a target of 10 kronor, and lowered Renault, Goodyear, Aumovio and Stabilus to neutral. Mercedes-Benz stays overweight, but its target falls to 50 euros from 70, with car margins seen at about 1%.

The bank expects global light-vehicle output to dip 0.2% in 2027, with China down 2%, North America down 1.5% and Europe up 2%. It also noted tyre raw-material costs were up 56% year on year in October.

The note landed on the same day European car shares rallied on the hybrid quota news, with BMW and Stellantis up about 5%. For suppliers, it signals that investors see more value in truck and premium programmes than in mass-market cars next year.
