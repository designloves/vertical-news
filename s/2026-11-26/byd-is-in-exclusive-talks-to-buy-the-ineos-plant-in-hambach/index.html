BYD is in exclusive talks to buy the Ineos plant in Hambach, France

A second Chinese carmaker taking over an existing European plant would speed up local production ahead of 'Made in Europe' rules.

BYD is reported to be in exclusive negotiations to buy Ineos Automotive's factory in Hambach, in north-eastern France near the German border. Ineos plans to move production of the Grenadier and Quartermaster to the US from 2028, since the US accounts for roughly 70% of its sales and local production avoids the 25% tariff on imported pickups.

Ineos bought the site from Mercedes-Benz in 2020 and says it has invested more than 700 million euros there. The plant employs 900 to 1,000 people and can build about 30,000 vehicles a year. If the sale goes through, Ineos would keep building its models at Hambach for the medium term until US output starts. No financial terms were given.

BYD's plant in Szeged, Hungary, is moving towards mass production with initial capacity of about 150,000 cars. A French site would add capacity inside the EU as hybrid quotas and local-content rules tighten. Chery bought Nissan's former Barcelona plant in 2024.
