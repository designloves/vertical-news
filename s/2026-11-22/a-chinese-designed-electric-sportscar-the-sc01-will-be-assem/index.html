A Chinese-designed electric sportscar, the SC01, will be assembled in Italy from May 2027

It is another small sign of Chinese EV designs being built inside the EU, partly to avoid tariffs and handle homologation locally.

Italian company FIPA (Fabbrica Italiana Produzione Autoveicoli) plans to assemble the SC01 electric sportscar in the Marche region from the end of May 2027. The car was developed by Chinese startup Tianjin Gongjiangpai Auto Technology. FIPA is its only authorised importer and maker for the EU and is responsible for European homologation. The Chinese version has 320 kW, all-wheel drive, weighs just under 1.4 tonnes and reaches 100 km/h in 2.9 seconds. European specifications are not yet known.

The first phase uses an existing industrial site with 2,000 m² of production space, growing to 24,000 m² within two years. Buildings will be renovated from January to March 2027, with lines and homologation completed by May. About 50 people will work in the first phase, rising to 285 by the end of 2030. FIPA also plans light EVs and its own models. Investment and volumes were not disclosed.

The project is small, but it follows the same route as larger Chinese brands seeking European plants.
