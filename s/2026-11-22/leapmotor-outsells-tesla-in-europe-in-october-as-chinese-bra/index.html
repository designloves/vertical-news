Leapmotor outsells Tesla in Europe in October as Chinese brands double their volume

Chinese brands are scaling fast in Europe through Stellantis channels and hybrids, even as their share dips.

Preliminary Dataforce figures covering about 98% of the European market show Leapmotor registered 13,902 cars in October, up 256%, ahead of Tesla's 11,722. Tesla's sales are concentrated in the last month of each quarter. The overall market rose 3.3% to about 1.11 million cars, and battery-electric cars grew 57% to a 31% share, while petrol fell 18% and diesel 26%.

Chinese brands more than doubled their combined volume, but their share slipped to 13.5% from a record 14.6% in September. Geely jumped to 8,144 cars and Chery grew 290%. Polestar was the only major Chinese-owned brand to fall, down 30% to 2,659. Chinese brands also registered 52,467 plug-in hybrids, up 182%, likely pulled forward before an EU cap of 350,000 Chinese hybrids a year starts in December.

For European OEMs and suppliers, the numbers show competition now coming through hybrids and partnerships as well as pure EVs. Official ACEA figures follow later this month.
