UK haulier Culina orders 1,051 Volvo FH Aero trucks

A large diesel order for Volvo shows fleets still buying efficiency upgrades rather than switching to electric.

British logistics group Culina has ordered 1,051 Volvo FH Aero trucks, after putting 134 Volvo FH trucks into service in 2024, Dutch trade magazine NT reports. The trucks will arrive in three batches, with all on the road by the second quarter of 2027, and each is expected to cover between 150,000 and 260,000 kilometres a year.

The order is for diesel trucks with Volvo's D13TC turbo-compound engine, which adds 300 Nm of torque and recovers energy from exhaust gas, combined with the I-Shift automated gearbox and predictive cruise control. The FH Aero's longer, more aerodynamic cab is designed to cut fuel use on long-haul routes.

The order has so far been reported by a single trade source, without a Volvo Group release. For drivetrain engineers it is a reminder that high-mileage diesel powertrains, with their gearbox and bearing loads, will make up a large part of European truck fleets for years, even as electric truck sales grow.
