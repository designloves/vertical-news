Persona AI ships welding humanoids to HD Hyundai shipyards, where skilled welders are training them

Humanoids are being taught trade skills on existing equipment, a model that could reach heavy fabrication in vehicle plants.

US robot maker Persona AI has shipped its bipedal humanoid robots to Korea, where HD Hyundai's shipbuilding and robotics units are developing their welding functions. Skilled welders in the US and Korea are training the robots. The partners signed a development agreement in March, built second-generation robots in May and passed enterprise-grade quality validation in September. Commercial use is expected around the end of the year, with a first site planned.

HD Hyundai is responsible for integration, welding-quality technology and staged rollout. A key design goal is that the robot works with existing welding gear built for people, including a 15 kg wire feeder, rather than needing a rebuilt workcell.

For manufacturing engineers, shipyard welding is a demanding test of mobile robots in unstructured, cramped spaces. If the approach works, similar robots could take on low-volume welding and repair tasks in truck, bus and body-in-white production, where fixed robot cells are hard to justify.
