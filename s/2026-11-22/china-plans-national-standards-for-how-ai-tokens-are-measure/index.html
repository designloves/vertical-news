China plans national standards for how AI tokens are measured, billed and quality-checked

Tokens are becoming a regulated unit of sale, which could shape how AI features are priced and compared.

China's National Data Administration said on Saturday that it will improve standards for measuring, billing and testing the quality of AI tokens. Director Liu Liehong also wants to widen the uses of token services and curb low-quality duplication as so-called token factories spread. These bundle rented compute, models and data into one service. No date was given for the standards.

The agency's figures show how fast use is growing. Daily token use in China passed 140 trillion in March, over 1,000 times more than in early 2024, and passed 500 trillion by June. Banks and telecom operators already sell tokens to consumers, so the rules would reach retail buyers as well as businesses.

Most AI pricing today is per token, but providers count tokens differently, so the same task costs different amounts. A government standard would be a first step toward comparing them like for like. Product managers pricing AI features should watch whether other regions follow.
